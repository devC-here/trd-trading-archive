================================================================================
  MODEL OUTPUT REFERENCE GUIDE
  5-Model Ensemble: Breakout · Candle · Microstructure · Momentum · VWAP
================================================================================
  How to read this file
  ---------------------
  Each model predicts 6 heads. Every head fires one class label per bar.
  For each class you will find:
    - What it means in plain English
    - What it implies for the next 1-2 candles
    - What action / alert it suggests

  ATR threshold reference (used by all label logic):
    small move  = net 2-bar move < 0.30 × ATR
    medium move = 0.40 – 1.20 × ATR
    large move  = > 1.20 × ATR
    seq_range   = (2-bar high − 2-bar low) / ATR

  trade_active flag (present on ALL 5 predictors):
    True  = directional head is NOT neutral/flat  AND  confirmation head >= partial
    False = model is unsure — stand aside
================================================================================

  MODEL 1 — BREAKOUT / SQUEEZE
  File   : breakout_deploy.py
  Purpose: Detects Bollinger-band squeezes and predicts the explosive release
  Input  : 27 squeeze / band / volatility features over ~120 bars
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  HEAD 0 — next_direction
  ═══════════════════════
  Label      | Meaning
  -----------+--------------------------------------------------------------
  bearish    | Net 2-bar close will move DOWN by > 0.30 × ATR.
             | The squeeze (or current compression) is resolving to the downside.
  flat       | Net 2-bar move is WITHIN ±0.30 × ATR.
             | No clear directional release yet; band is still coiling or
             | the move is too small to classify as directional.
  bullish    | Net 2-bar close will move UP by > 0.30 × ATR.
             | The squeeze is releasing to the upside.

  Implication: This is the PRIMARY signal. Use with bk_follow_through to
  confirm. If flat, do not trade this model — wait for the next bar.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 1 — next_magnitude
  ════════════════════════
  Label      | ATR threshold | Meaning
  -----------+---------------+----------------------------------------------
  small      | < 0.40 × ATR  | The coming move is tiny — body < 40% of ATR.
             |               | Squeeze may not have fully released yet, or
             |               | is a false breakout with no follow-through.
  medium     | 0.40–1.20 ATR | Normal-sized candle. A genuine but not
             |               | explosive breakout move. Typical scalp range.
  large      | > 1.20 × ATR  | Explosive candle. Bands are expanding fast.
             |               | Expect a full-range move, possible gap-and-go.

  Implication: Combine with next_direction. bullish + large = strong buy
  signal. bearish + small = weak move, tight stop or skip.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 2 — next_pattern
  ══════════════════════
  Label         | Meaning
  --------------+-----------------------------------------------------------
  continuation  | Candle 2 continues in the same direction as candle 1.
                | The breakout has momentum behind it — two-bar follow-through.
  reversal      | Candle 2 reverses candle 1's direction with >= 80% body strength.
                | Breakout was a fake — price snapped back. Watch for trap entry.
  indecision    | Both candles are small, OR candle 2 weakly reverses candle 1.
                | Market is still digesting the squeeze release; no clean read yet.

  Implication: continuation = full-size position. reversal = avoid or
  fade the breakout. indecision = wait for the next bar for confirmation.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 3 — next_volatility
  ═════════════════════════
  Label       | 2-bar seq_range | Meaning
  ------------+-----------------+--------------------------------------------
  contracting | < 1.0 × ATR     | The 2-bar range is SHRINKING vs recent ATR.
              |                 | Squeeze has NOT yet released. Patience needed.
  normal      | 1.0–2.5 × ATR   | Normal volatility release. On-par with average.
  expanding   | > 2.5 × ATR     | Volatility EXPLOSION. Band walk / trend day
              |                 | likely forming. Widen targets, trail stop.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 4 — next_close_loc
  ════════════════════════
  Label        | close_gravity | Meaning
  -------------+---------------+---------------------------------------------
  lower_third  | < 0.33        | Candle 2 closes in LOWER 1/3 of its range.
               |               | Bears dominating; selling pressure into close.
  mid          | 0.33–0.67     | Candle closes in the MIDDLE. Balanced or
               |               | indecision. Price accepted but no dominance.
  upper_third  | > 0.67        | Candle 2 closes in UPPER 1/3 of its range.
               |               | Bulls dominating; buying pressure into close.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 5 — bk_follow_through
  ═══════════════════════════
  Label    | Meaning
  ---------+----------------------------------------------------------------
  no       | Price DID NOT follow through in the expected breakout direction.
           | Breakout failed. If you entered on the signal, exit quickly.
  partial  | Price moved somewhat in the expected direction (> 0.10 × ATR)
           | but did NOT reach a strong move (< 0.60 × ATR).
           | Partial success — take partial profit, trail remainder.
  yes      | Full confirmation. Price moved strongly (> 0.60 × ATR) in the
           | expected breakout direction. Breakout is genuine.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  BREAKOUT DECISION ALGORITHM
  ═══════════════════════════
  Step 1 — Gate check
    trade_active == True?   → proceed
    trade_active == False?  → skip this bar

  Step 2 — Direction
    next_direction == bullish?  → look to buy
    next_direction == bearish?  → look to sell
    next_direction == flat?     → no trade

  Step 3 — Quality filter (apply ALL three for highest confidence)
    next_magnitude     != small       (want at least a medium move)
    next_pattern       == continuation
    bk_follow_through  >= partial

  Step 4 — Size your trade
    next_magnitude == large  AND  next_volatility == expanding  → full size
    next_magnitude == medium AND  bk_follow_through == partial  → half size
    anything else                                               → skip

  Step 5 — Exit hint
    next_close_loc == upper_third (for longs) → hold, trend day forming
    next_close_loc == mid                      → take profit at first target
    next_close_loc == lower_third (for longs)  → aggressive stop; possible reversal

  MODEL 2 — CANDLE SEQUENCE
  File   : candle_deploy.py
  Purpose: Reads 10-bar candlestick sequences, predicts next-candle anatomy
  Input  : 30 pattern / sequence / Heikin Ashi features
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  HEAD 0 — next_direction
  ═══════════════════════
  Label    | ATR threshold | Meaning
  ---------+---------------+--------------------------------------------------
  bearish  | < -0.30 ATR   | Next candle closes DOWN — body is net negative.
  doji     | ±0.30 ATR     | Next candle has almost no body. Transition candle.
           |               | Often appears at pivots or before large moves.
  bullish  | > +0.30 ATR   | Next candle closes UP — body is net positive.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 1 — next_body_size
  ════════════════════════
  Label  | avg body (ATR) | Meaning
  -------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------
  small  | < 0.40         | Doji / spinning top — nearly no net body.
         |                | Very low conviction, watch for reversal signal.
  normal | 0.40–1.20      | Standard candle body. Trend day in progress or
         |                | normal price action within a sequence.
  large  | > 1.20         | Marubozu-style candle. Strong single-bar conviction.
         |                | High probability the direction is genuine.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 2 — next_pattern
  ══════════════════════
  Label       | Meaning
  ------------+---------------------------------------------------------------
  impulse     | Standard directional candle. No dominant wick; body is the
              | story. The 10-bar sequence is driving normal trend behavior.
  pin         | Pin bar / hammer / shooting star detected.
              | Lower wick > 40% of range (bull pin) or upper wick (bear pin).
              | High probability reversal setup from a key level.
  inside_doji | Doji or inside bar forming. Body < 12% of ATR.
              | Market is in equilibrium — compression before a move.
  engulf      | Engulfing candle detected. Candle 1 body > candle 2 body × 1.3
              | AND body > 0.80 ATR. Strong single-bar reversal or acceleration.

  Implication: pin → look for reversal from S/R. engulf → take the direction
  of the engulf. inside_doji → wait for the breakout of the inside bar range.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 3 — seq_continuation
  ══════════════════════════
  Label        | Meaning
  -------------+--------------------------------------------------------------
  reversal     | The 10-bar sequence context (Heikin Ashi trend + directional
               | ratio) is bullish/bearish, but next_direction opposes it.
               | A reversal is forming — the sequence is changing character.
  indecision   | No clear HA trend context, OR next_direction is doji.
               | Sequence has mixed signals; stand aside or reduce size.
  continuation | Next_direction MATCHES the sequence context (HA trend + dir ratio).
               | The existing move is continuing. Trend trade opportunity.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 4 — next_close_grav
  ═════════════════════════
  Label        | close gravity | Meaning
  -------------+---------------+----------------------------------------------
  lower_third  | < 0.33        | Next candle closes near its LOW. Bearish close.
               |               | Sellers controlled the full bar; overhead supply.
  mid          | 0.33–0.67     | Next candle closes in the middle of the bar.
               |               | Balanced action; no clear end-of-bar pressure.
  upper_third  | > 0.67        | Next candle closes near its HIGH. Bullish close.
               |               | Buyers pushed price up and held all gains.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 5 — next_volatility
  ═════════════════════════
  Label       | seq_range (ATR) | Meaning
  ------------+-----------------+---------------------------------------------
  compression | < 0.90          | 2-bar range is TIGHTENING vs ATR. 
              |                 | Coiling sequence forming; energy building.
  normal      | 0.90–2.50       | Volatility in line with recent average.
  expansion   | > 2.50          | Volatility BURST coming. Widen stops/targets.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CANDLE DECISION ALGORITHM
  ═════════════════════════
  Step 1 — Gate check
    trade_active == True?  → proceed
    trade_active == False? → skip (direction is doji OR seq is indecision)

  Step 2 — Direction + confirmation
    next_direction  == bullish  AND  seq_continuation == continuation → buy
    next_direction  == bearish  AND  seq_continuation == continuation → sell
    seq_continuation == reversal → look for reversal trade in OPPOSITE direction

  Step 3 — Entry quality
    next_pattern == pin      → reversal entry from key level (counter-trend)
    next_pattern == engulf   → strong momentum entry (trend-continuation)
    next_pattern == impulse  → standard trend entry
    next_pattern == inside_doji → wait for break of inside bar before entry

  Step 4 — Target / exit
    next_close_grav == upper_third (longs)  → trail stop, buyers still in control
    next_close_grav == lower_third (longs)  → take profit quickly, supply overhead
    next_volatility == expansion            → widen targets by 50%
    next_volatility == compression          → tighter targets, range-bound

  MODEL 3 — MICROSTRUCTURE
  File   : microstructure_deploy.py
  Purpose: Reads single-bar anatomy + order-flow proxies to predict bar intent
  Input  : 32 tick-delta / wick / orderflow / body features
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  HEAD 0 — next_flow_dir
  ══════════════════════
  Label     | Meaning
  ----------+-----------------------------------------------------------------
  bearish   | Net 2-bar close < -0.30 × ATR. Sell-side orderflow is winning.
            | Tick delta and cumulative delta both negative.
  contested | Net 2-bar move within ±0.30 × ATR. Buyers and sellers are
            | equally active. Orderflow is ambiguous — no edge yet.
  bullish   | Net 2-bar close > +0.30 × ATR. Buy-side orderflow is winning.

  Implication: This is the single-bar orderflow version of "direction."
  Unlike the Breakout model, it is based on tick-by-tick pressure, not price.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 1 — next_bar_intent
  ═════════════════════════
  Label      | Meaning
  -----------+----------------------------------------------------------------
  absorption | Candle 1 has tiny body (< 0.15 ATR) AND large combined wicks
             | (> 0.50 of range). Both sides fought to a standstill.
             | Large players are absorbing supply/demand. Often precedes reversal.
  rejection  | One wick > 40% of range while opposite wick < 20%.
             | Market attempted to move one way and was STRONGLY REJECTED.
             | Very high probability reversal from this level.
  impulse    | Large body (> 0.80 ATR) and close in upper/lower third.
             | Strong single-bar momentum with almost no counter-pressure.
  fade       | Candle 2 body is < 60% of candle 1 body. Move is running out
             | of steam — momentum is decelerating. Expect a stall or reversal.

  Implication: rejection → fade the attempted direction (trade the bounce).
  impulse → trade WITH the candle direction. absorption → wait for next bar.
  fade → exit existing position or tighten stop.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 2 — next_magnitude
  ════════════════════════
  Label  | avg body (ATR) | Meaning
  -------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------
  thin   | < 0.50         | Very small candle body. Low conviction bar.
         |                | Often seen in absorption, ranging, or pre-news.
  normal | 0.50–1.50      | Standard-sized bar. Typical intraday behavior.
  strong | > 1.50         | Very large body. Momentum candle.
         |                | Confirms impulse intent, trail stop aggressively.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 3 — next_rejection
  ═════════════════════════
  Label   | wick ratio | Meaning
  --------+------------+-------------------------------------------------------
  none    | < 0.20     | No meaningful wick in the opposing direction.
          |            | Clean candle body — conviction is high.
  partial | 0.20–0.45  | Moderate opposing wick. Some pushback at the extreme.
          |            | Slightly reduces conviction; tighten target.
  strong  | > 0.45     | Large opposing wick — market strongly rejected the
          |            | extreme. Even if the body is large, the wick warns of
          |            | a potential reversal on the next bar.

  Note: For a bull bar, rejection is the UPPER wick (highs being rejected).
        For a bear bar, rejection is the LOWER wick (lows being rejected).

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 4 — momentum_follow
  ═════════════════════════
  Label     | Meaning
  ----------+-----------------------------------------------------------------
  failed    | Orderflow context (tick_delta + cum_delta_5z) was directional,
            | but price DID NOT follow. Signal failed.
            | This is a WARNING — the orderflow signal was a fake-out.
  partial   | Price moved somewhat in the orderflow direction but < 0.60 ATR.
            | Partial follow-through. Take partial profit, trail remainder.
  confirmed | Price moved > 0.60 ATR in the direction the orderflow predicted.
            | Full confirmation. Orderflow model is working on this bar.

  Note: When no clear orderflow bias exists, this head defaults to "partial"
  (neutral reading, not a strong signal either way).

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 5 — next_range_type
  ═════════════════════════
  Label            | Meaning
  -----------------+----------------------------------------------------------
  inside_compress  | 2-bar range < 70% of last bar's range. Market is COILING
                   | inside the previous bar. Energy building for a breakout.
  normal           | 2-bar range between 70–150% of last bar's range.
                   | Normal continuation bar. No range anomaly.
  outside_expand   | 2-bar range > 150% of last bar's range.
                   | Outside bar / volatility expansion. Widen stops/targets.
                   | This often traps traders who fade the first direction.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MICROSTRUCTURE DECISION ALGORITHM
  ══════════════════════════════════
  Step 1 — Gate check
    trade_active == True?   → proceed (flow_dir != contested AND follow >= partial)
    trade_active == False?  → skip

  Step 2 — Intent filter (most important head after flow_dir)
    next_bar_intent == impulse    → trade WITH flow_dir direction
    next_bar_intent == rejection  → trade AGAINST the rejected direction (fade it)
    next_bar_intent == absorption → stand aside, wait for next bar clarity
    next_bar_intent == fade       → exit existing positions, do not enter new

  Step 3 — Rejection check (kill switch)
    next_rejection == strong  → even if impulse signal, reduce size by 50%
                                because the wick warns of reversal

  Step 4 — Range context
    next_range_type == inside_compress → prepare for breakout, set alerts
    next_range_type == outside_expand  → be cautious — volatility spike may
                                         reverse after initial move

  Step 5 — Confirmation
    momentum_follow == confirmed  → full size
    momentum_follow == partial    → half size
    momentum_follow == failed     → DO NOT enter; orderflow reading unreliable

  MODEL 4 — MOMENTUM
  File   : momentum_deploy.py
  Purpose: Multi-timeframe momentum assessment with Ichimoku + ADX + Donchian
  Input  : 30 trend / ADX / Ichimoku / Donchian / displacement features
           Requires 120+ bars for full indicator warmup
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  HEAD 0 — next_direction
  ═══════════════════════
  Label   | net_atr threshold | Meaning
  --------+-------------------+------------------------------------------------
  bearish | < -0.40           | Net 2-bar close will be DOWN > 0.40 × ATR.
          |                   | Momentum regime is shifting or continuing bearish.
  flat    | ±0.40             | Net move will be inside ±0.40 ATR.
          |                   | Momentum is stalling or choppy. No trend.
  bullish | > +0.40           | Net 2-bar close will be UP > 0.40 × ATR.
          |                   | Momentum regime is bullish.

  Note: The Momentum model uses a ±0.40 ATR threshold (wider than other models)
  because it needs to see larger moves to classify a momentum trend as real.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 1 — next_magnitude
  ════════════════════════
  Label  | avg body (ATR) | Meaning
  -------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------
  small  | < 0.50         | Move is sub-ATR. Momentum exists but the move is
         |                | not explosive. Standard trend continuation bar.
  large  | > 1.50         | Explosive multi-ATR bar. Strong momentum burst.
         |                | Breakout-quality move within the momentum regime.

  NOTE: This head has only 2 classes (not 3). There is no "medium."
  Any bar between 0.50 and 1.50 ATR is also classified as "small" here.
  This head specifically flags EXCEPTIONAL moves only.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 2 — next_regime_behaviour
  ════════════════════════════════
  Label        | Meaning
  -------------+--------------------------------------------------------------
  chop         | Both future candles are small (< 0.30 ATR body) AND 2-bar
               | range < 1.2 ATR. Market has NO directional intent.
               | ADX is likely low. Do not trade trend strategies here.
  pullback     | Candle 2 REVERSES candle 1 with >= 70% relative body size.
               | The trend is healthy but pausing to retest. This is your
               | entry point in a running trend — wait for the pullback low.
  continuation | Both candles are in the SAME direction with candle 2 >= 75%
               | body of candle 1, AND 2-bar range <= 2.5 ATR.
               | Classic trend continuation. Enter on small retracements.
  breakout     | Both candles same direction + relative body >= 75% + 
               | 2-bar range > 2.5 ATR. Something exceptional is happening.
               | News event, institutional flow, or technical breakout.
               | Widen targets massively; do NOT fade this.

  Implication: chop = no trade. pullback = entry zone. continuation = add
  to existing. breakout = run maximum size with wide stop.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 3 — next_volatility
  ═════════════════════════
  Label       | seq_range (ATR) | Meaning
  ------------+-----------------+---------------------------------------------
  contracting | < 1.0           | Momentum is SLOWING. Trend may be pausing
              |                 | or exhausting. Reduce position size.
  normal      | 1.0–2.5         | Momentum continues at a steady pace.
  expanding   | > 2.5           | Volatility surge within the trend.
              |                 | Acceleration phase — trend may be entering
              |                 | its final fast leg. Trail stops aggressively.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 4 — next_structural_bias
  ══════════════════════════════
  Label   | Condition                          | Meaning
  --------+------------------------------------+-------------------------------
  bearish | close_grav < 0.35 AND net_atr < 0  | After c2, price will be
          |                                    | structurally BELOW midpoint of
          |                                    | the 2-bar range. Bears in control.
  neutral | mixed close_grav and net_atr       | Price will be in the middle
          |                                    | of the range. No structural edge.
  bullish | close_grav > 0.65 AND net_atr > 0  | After c2, price will be
          |                                    | structurally ABOVE midpoint.
          |                                    | Bulls in control of structure.

  Implication: Use to validate next_direction. If next_direction == bullish
  but next_structural_bias == bearish — conflict flag. Reduce size.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 5 — next_momentum_quality
  ════════════════════════════════
  Label        | Condition                      | Meaning
  -------------+--------------------------------+-----------------------------
  fading       | c2 body < 60% of c1 body OR    | Momentum is DYING.
               | c2 reverses c1 direction       | Do not chase this move.
               |                                | If in a trade, tighten stop.
  stable       | c2 body = 60–120% of c1 body,  | Momentum is STEADY.
               | same direction                 | Standard trend day behavior.
               |                                | Hold existing position.
  accelerating | c2 body > 120% of c1 body AND  | Momentum is BUILDING.
               | same direction as c1           | Each bar is bigger than last.
               |                                | This is trend acceleration —
               |                                | add to position or full size.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MOMENTUM DECISION ALGORITHM
  ═══════════════════════════
  Step 1 — Gate check (all 3 must be true)
    next_direction          != flat          (something is moving)
    next_regime_behaviour   != chop          (not a ranging market)
    next_momentum_quality   != fading        (momentum still alive)
    trade_active == True    (covers all the above automatically)

  Step 2 — Regime action
    chop         → NO TRADE on any model; override everything
    pullback     → look to BUY the dip in a bullish trend
    continuation → standard trend-follow entry
    breakout     → maximum size, wide stop, large target

  Step 3 — Quality scoring (3 points = ideal, 0 = avoid)
    +1 if next_structural_bias matches next_direction
    +1 if next_momentum_quality == accelerating
    +1 if next_volatility == expanding or normal (not contracting)

    Score 3: full size
    Score 2: standard size
    Score 1: half size
    Score 0: stand aside

  Step 4 — Exit signal
    next_momentum_quality == fading       → exit immediately
    next_momentum_quality == stable       → trail stop, hold
    next_momentum_quality == accelerating → hold, widen stop

  MODEL 5 — VWAP / VOLUME
  File   : vwap_deploy.py
  Purpose: Institutional-flow analysis relative to rolling 50-bar VWAP
  Input  : 32 VWAP-deviation / volume-delta / session features
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  HEAD 0 — next_vwap_dir
  ══════════════════════
  Label          | Meaning
  ---------------+------------------------------------------------------------
  below_away     | Price will move DOWN and AWAY from VWAP.
                 | net_atr < -0.35 AND candle 2 closes below VWAP.
                 | Institutions may be distributing. Bears in control of VWAP.
  oscillate_near | Price stays NEAR VWAP (within ±0.35 ATR net).
                 | VWAP is acting as a magnet. Market is in balance.
                 | Avoid directional trades; fade extremes instead.
  above_away     | Price will move UP and AWAY from VWAP.
                 | net_atr > +0.35 AND candle 2 closes above VWAP.
                 | Institutions may be accumulating. Bulls in control of VWAP.

  Implication: This is the PRIMARY signal. Use with vwap_follow_through.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 1 — next_vol_char
  ══════════════════════
  Label    | avg body (ATR) | Meaning
  ---------+----------------+---------------------------------------------------
  declining| < 0.30         | Volume / body is DRYING UP. Move may be stalling.
           |                | Low-volume extension of a prior move — be cautious.
  stable   | 0.30–1.20      | Volume is NORMAL for current conditions.
           |                | Steady institutional participation.
  surge    | > 1.20         | Volume SPIKE detected. Institutional order flow is
           |                | active. Large players are moving in size.
           |                | High-conviction directional move likely.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 2 — vwap_interaction
  ══════════════════════════
  Label           | Meaning
  ----------------+-----------------------------------------------------------
  rejection       | Price approached VWAP from above but FAILED to reclaim it.
                  | VWAP is now resistance. Price closes below VWAP.
                  | Bearish context — institutions are defending VWAP as supply.
  test_return     | Price tested VWAP and is oscillating near it.
                  | Neither side has committed. Wait for a resolution.
  reclaim_hold    | Price CROSSED above VWAP and is HOLDING above it.
                  | Strong close + decent body confirms the reclaim.
                  | Bullish context — VWAP flipped to support.
  walk_extension  | Price is ABOVE VWAP and moving further away (net_atr > 0.50).
                  | Classic "VWAP band walk" — strong trend day.
                  | Institutions are aggressively buying above VWAP.

  Implication: reclaim_hold → long above VWAP with VWAP as stop.
  rejection → short below VWAP with VWAP as stop.
  walk_extension → do not fade, trail stop below VWAP.
  test_return → wait for resolution before entering.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 3 — next_inst_signal
  ══════════════════════════
  Label        | Condition                        | Meaning
  -------------+----------------------------------+---------------------------
  distribution | avg body > 0.80 ATR, range > 1.5 | Large bars with net
               | ATR, but net_atr <= 0.3          | bearish close. Institutions
               |                                  | are SELLING into strength.
               |                                  | Bearish divergence.
  neutral      | thin bars (body < 0.25 ATR)      | No strong signal.
               | OR mixed conditions              | Absorption or consolidation.
  accumulation | avg body > 0.80 ATR, range > 1.5 | Large bars with strong
               | ATR, AND net_atr > 0.30          | bullish close. Institutions
               |                                  | are BUYING. Bullish flow.

  Implication: accumulation + above_away = very strong long signal.
  distribution + below_away = very strong short signal.
  Always cross-check with next_vol_char: surge + accumulation = best signal.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 4 — next_close_location
  ══════════════════════════════
  Label        | close gravity | Meaning
  -------------+---------------+----------------------------------------------
  lower_third  | < 0.33        | Candle 2 closes near its LOW.
               |               | Selling pressure controlled close. Bearish.
  mid          | 0.33–0.67     | Candle closes in the MIDDLE of its range.
               |               | No strong end-of-bar directional pressure.
  upper_third  | > 0.67        | Candle 2 closes near its HIGH.
               |               | Buying pressure controlled close. Bullish.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HEAD 5 — vwap_follow_through
  ═════════════════════════════
  Label     | Meaning
  ----------+-----------------------------------------------------------------
  no        | Price did NOT follow the VWAP signal direction.
            | VWAP trade failed. The signal was a false cross.
  partial   | Price moved somewhat (> 0.15 ATR) in the signal direction
            | but < 0.60 ATR. Take partial profit, trail remainder tightly.
  confirmed | Price moved > 0.60 ATR in the direction the VWAP context
            | predicted. Full institutional follow-through confirmed.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  VWAP DECISION ALGORITHM
  ═══════════════════════
  Step 1 — Gate check
    trade_active == True?   → proceed (vwap_dir != oscillate_near AND follow >= partial)
    trade_active == False?  → skip

  Step 2 — VWAP location setup
    vwap_interaction == reclaim_hold    → buy above VWAP, stop below VWAP
    vwap_interaction == rejection       → sell below VWAP, stop above VWAP
    vwap_interaction == walk_extension  → trail existing long above VWAP
    vwap_interaction == test_return     → wait for next bar before committing

  Step 3 — Institutional confirmation (optional but powerful)
    next_inst_signal == accumulation  AND  next_vwap_dir == above_away → STRONG BUY
    next_inst_signal == distribution  AND  next_vwap_dir == below_away → STRONG SELL
    next_inst_signal == neutral                                         → normal size

  Step 4 — Volume quality
    next_vol_char == surge    → full size
    next_vol_char == stable   → standard size
    next_vol_char == declining → reduce size by 50%; move may stall soon

  Step 5 — Exit hint
    next_close_location == upper_third (longs)  → hold, buyers still in charge
    next_close_location == lower_third (longs)  → take profit, supply near close
    vwap_follow_through == confirmed            → hold, all-in
    vwap_follow_through == no                   → exit immediately


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ENSEMBLE COMBINING ALGORITHM
  Using all 5 models together for maximum-confidence signals
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Rule 1 — MASTER GATE (must pass before doing anything)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Count models with trade_active == True.
    5 active → exceptional setup, maximum size
    4 active → high confidence, full size
    3 active → standard size
    2 active → half size or skip
    0–1 active → DO NOT TRADE on any model this bar

  Rule 2 — DIRECTION CONSENSUS
  ─────────────────────────────
  Assign each active model's directional score:
    bullish / above_away / bullish flow  → +1
    bearish / below_away / bearish flow  → -1
    flat / oscillate / contested         →  0

  Sum of scores:
    +3 to +5  → strong BUY signal
    +1 to +2  → mild BUY, reduce size
     0        → conflicted, no trade
    -1 to -2  → mild SELL, reduce size
    -3 to -5  → strong SELL signal

  Rule 3 — REGIME CHECK (Momentum model overrides all)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  If momentum.next_regime_behaviour == chop → VETO all trades this bar
  If momentum.next_regime_behaviour == breakout → IGNORE size limits, max size
  If momentum.next_regime_behaviour == pullback → only enter on retracement setups

  Rule 4 — VOLATILITY ALIGNMENT
  ──────────────────────────────
  Count models predicting expanding volatility:
    3+ models expanding → widen all stops and targets by 50%
    3+ models contracting → tighten targets, this may be a fake move

  Rule 5 — CONFIRMATION TIER (follow-through heads)
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Breakout  : bk_follow_through    confirmed = 2 pts, partial = 1 pt, no = 0 pts
  Candle    : seq_continuation     continuation = 2 pts, indecision = 0 pts
  Micro     : momentum_follow      confirmed = 2 pts, partial = 1 pt, failed = -2 pts
  Momentum  : next_momentum_quality accelerating = 2 pts, stable = 1 pt, fading = -2 pts
  VWAP      : vwap_follow_through   confirmed = 2 pts, partial = 1 pt, no = 0 pts

  Total confirmation score (max = 10):
    8–10 → hold maximum size, trail aggressively
    5–7  → standard hold
    3–4  → take partial profit
    0–2  → exit or do not enter

  Rule 6 — CONFLICT / KILL SWITCH (override any long/short signal)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  EXIT or DO NOT ENTER if any of these fire:
    micro.next_bar_intent     == absorption  (large players absorbing, direction unclear)
    micro.momentum_follow     == failed      (orderflow signal fake-out)
    momentum.next_regime      == chop        (market has no trend)
    momentum.next_momentum_quality == fading (trend dying)
    breakout.next_pattern     == reversal    (breakout failed)
    vwap.vwap_interaction     == rejection   (opposing the trade direction)
