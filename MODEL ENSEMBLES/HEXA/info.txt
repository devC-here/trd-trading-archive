
✅ FRAMA Model Output Guide
Your model is producing multi-target classifications, where each key represents a distinct analytical component of the market. Here's how to understand and use each one.

1. 🧭 frama_trend: Overall Market Trend
Purpose: Indicates whether the market is trending up, down, or sideways based on the FRAMA line (a fractal adaptive moving average).

Value	Meaning	Trading Insight
0	Downtrend	Look for short setups, avoid aggressive buys.
1	Sideways	Consolidation phase — consider range strategies.
2	Uptrend	Look for long setups, avoid aggressive shorts.

How to use: This is a high-level filter. Only trade in the direction of the trend unless strong reversal signals appear.

2. 💪 frama_strength: Trend Strength
Purpose: Measures how dominant or mature the current trend is.

Value	Meaning	Trading Insight
0	Weak	Trend is forming or fading — use caution.
1	Medium	Trend is reliable but may need confirmation.
2	Strong	Confirms that price is moving with momentum.

How to use: Strong trends favor continuation trades. Weak trends often precede reversals or fakeouts.

3. 🌪️ volatility_bucket: Volatility Level
Purpose: Classifies market activity based on price fluctuations (range/ATR/spreads).

Value	Meaning	Trading Insight
0	Low	Market is calm — be careful of false breakouts.
1	Medium	Balanced — most systems work reliably here.
2	High	Fast markets — use wider stops, seek momentum.

How to use: Adjust stop-loss and take-profit levels dynamically. High volatility favors breakout systems, while low favors mean-reversion.

4. 🧮 position_relative: Price Position vs FRAMA Line
Purpose: Shows where the price is relative to the FRAMA — this gives reversion or breakout signals.

Value	Meaning	Trading Insight
0	Below FRAMA	Bearish pressure, possible shorts.
1	Near FRAMA	Market equilibrium — avoid big decisions yet.
2	Above FRAMA	Bullish sentiment, possible longs.

How to use: Combines well with frama_trend — price far from FRAMA often means overstretched or trending strongly.

5. 🔮 future_sim_direction: Predicted Near-Term Direction
Purpose: Predicts price direction over a short simulated horizon using features from recent data.

Value	Meaning	Trading Insight
0	Down	Expect short-term drop
1	Up	Expect short-term rally

How to use: Align this with the frama_trend and position_relative to confirm entries.

📘 Model Understanding Guide
Your model has three heads — each outputs a classification relevant to a key technical indicator:

🎯 1. ADX Regime (adx_regime)
Represents trend strength:

Class	Meaning	Typical Action
0	Weak trend	Range-trading / caution
1	Moderate trend	Confirm with other signals
2	Strong trend	Trend-following OK (momentum)

Used to filter out false signals during weak trend periods.

📈 2. CCI Zone (cci_zone)
Represents momentum deviation from average price:

Class	Meaning	Typical Action
0	Oversold (< -100)	Consider Buy (rebound)
1	Neutral	Wait or confirm others
2	Overbought (> 100)	Consider Sell (reversal)

Used to assess short-term price extremes.

🔄 3. MACD Cross Flag (macd_cross_flag)
Represents momentum shifts via crossover:

Class	Meaning	Typical Action
0	Bearish Cross (Sell Signal)	Consider Selling
1	No Cross (Neutral)	No action / confirm
2	Bullish Cross (Buy Signal)	Consider Buying

Acts as your main directional trigger.

✅ Guide to Understanding Your 7 Signal Outputs
Each output your model produces represents a trader-observed market condition that provides context — not just a trade instruction. These are building blocks for tactical decisions.

1. 🎯 boll_position_class
Meaning: Where is the price relative to the Bollinger Bands?

Class	Trader Interpretation	Motive / Use
lower-out	Price has broken below lower band	Oversold / potential bounce area
lower-in	Price re-entered from lower edge	Reversal forming / weak downtrend
mean	Near mid-band	Market indecision / no strong setup
upper-in	Re-entered from above	Distribution forming / weakening bullishness
upper-out	Price broke above upper band	Overbought / potential short-term top

🔍 Use: This is your spatial context. Where are you in the volatility envelope?

2. 📉 squeeze_state
Meaning: What’s happening to the band width (volatility)?

Class	Interpretation	Motive / Use
squeeze	Bands are very narrow	Market is coiling — breakout likely
stable	Normal range	Regular price movement
expanding	Bands are widening	Volatility expansion — momentum surge likely

🔍 Use: Think of this as a volatility regime detector. Use it to anticipate explosions or exhaustion.

3. 📈 trend_regime
Meaning: What’s the current trend strength?

Class	Interpretation	Motive / Use
no_trend	Flat / range-bound	Avoid trend trades
weak_trend	Mild direction	Cautious entry zone
strong_trend	Conviction movement	Trend-following trades active

🔍 Use: Tells you if you should trade with momentum or play mean reversion. It’s a trend filter.

4. 🔁 momentum_zone
Meaning: Where does momentum bias lie (via RSI)?

Class	Interpretation	Motive / Use
bearish	RSI < 40	Downward bias — favor short setups
neutral	RSI 40–60	Unclear — avoid momentum trades
bullish	RSI > 60	Upward bias — favor long setups

🔍 Use: Confirms or rejects the direction of a trade. Align with trend_regime if possible.

5. ⚠️ reversal_signal
Meaning: Has price recently shown a reversal cue?

Class	Interpretation	Motive / Use
0	No reversal setup	Let market develop further
1	Reversal signal present (band incursion + MACD shift)	Prepare for rotation in direction

🔍 Use: Helps catch pivots. Often used after incursion or when exiting an exhausted trend.

6. 🔄 rejection_flag
Meaning: Did the price attempt a breakout but got rejected?

Class	Interpretation	Motive / Use
0	No rejection wick present	Breakout may be real
1	Wick / tail formed at band	Weak breakout — possible fade setup

🔍 Use: This is candle anatomy logic. Traders love seeing wicks at extremes. Suggests failed moves.

7. 🔒 compression_event
Meaning: Was there a tight price range recently?

Class	Interpretation	Motive / Use
0	No compression	Market moving normally
1	Recent tight consolidation	Likely to break soon — monitor closely

🔍 Use: Detects coiled conditions, similar to squeeze, but based on price range tightness, not just bands.
