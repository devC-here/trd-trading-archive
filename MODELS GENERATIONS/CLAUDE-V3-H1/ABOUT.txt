SECTION 1 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The system consists of 5 independent neural networks, each trained on a
different synthetic H1 FX dataset and each specialising in a different
"lens" of market behaviour. They share no weights and are meant to be
used together.

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                    LIVE H1 OHLCV BAR STREAM                         │
  └──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                             │  (same bars fed to all 5 simultaneously)
        ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                    ▼          ▼           ▼                  ▼
  ┌──────────┐        ┌──────────┐  ┌──────┐  ┌──────────┐  ┌──────────────┐
  │  VWAP /  │        │ MOMENTUM │  │MICRO-│  │  CANDLE  │  │  BREAKOUT /  │
  │  VOLUME  │        │          │  │STRUC-│  │ SEQUENCE │  │   SQUEEZE    │
  └────┬─────┘        └────┬─────┘  │TURE  │  └────┬─────┘  └──────┬───────┘
       │                   │        └──┬───┘        │               │
       │  6 outputs        │  6 out    │  6 out     │  6 out        │  6 out
       ▼                   ▼           ▼             ▼               ▼
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                    ENSEMBLE AGGREGATION LAYER                           │
  │          (weighted vote / confidence gate / signal consensus)           │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                      │
                                 FINAL SIGNAL


DATA FLOW PER MODEL
───────────────────
1. MT5 fetches OHLCV H1 bars (each model requests its own lookback window)
2. Deploy script computes features from raw OHLCV — no ML in features
3. Features are RobustScaler-normalised using the saved scaler (.pkl)
4. Scaled features are passed through the neural network
5. Each of 6 output heads produces a softmax probability distribution
6. The class with highest probability is the prediction; full prob vector
   is also available for soft ensembling

IMPORTANT: Every model uses TIMEFRAME_H1. Do not mix M15 bars in.

SECTION 2 — MARKET PHYSICS ENGINE (market_physics_h1.py)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All 5 synthetic datasets were generated using a shared physics engine that
makes the synthetic candles behave like real H1 FX data. Understanding this
helps interpret why the models are calibrated the way they are.

SESSION VOLUME PROFILE
  H1 volume is not uniform. The engine applies these multipliers:
  
  Hour (UTC)  Volume Multiplier   Session
  ──────────  ─────────────────   ──────────────────────────────
  00 – 05     0.20 – 0.30×        Deep Asia / early Pacific
  06 – 07     0.45 – 0.65×        Pre-London
  08 – 09     1.00 – 1.20×        London open  ← peak
  10 – 12     0.85 – 1.15×        London mid-session
  13 – 14     1.30 – 1.40×        New York open  ← peak
  15 – 17     0.80 – 1.20×        NY mid / London close
  18 – 23     0.28 – 0.45×        NY close / after-hours

  Implication: models learn that high-volume signals during 08–09 and 13–14
  carry more weight than the same signal at 02:00. The session_vol_factor
  feature in VWAP captures this. When running live, the hour is extracted
  from bar timestamps and feeds session-aware volume normalisation.

GARCH VOLATILITY CLUSTERING
  Body size of each candle is drawn from a gamma distribution scaled by a
  running GARCH(1,1) sigma: σ_t = ω + α|shock| + β·σ_{t-1}
  
  This means: after a large candle, the next 3–5 candles also tend to be
  large. After quiet periods, body sizes cluster small. This is exactly how
  real H1 FX behaves around news, session opens, and breakouts.
  
  Implication: the models have been trained on volatility-clustered sequences.
  They naturally learn that "expanding range" signals are more meaningful
  when they follow a compression phase.

AR(1) DIRECTIONAL BIAS
  The directional probability of each candle evolves via AR(1):
  bias_t = ar × bias_{t-1} + (1 − ar) × target + noise
  
  Trends build gradually and fade gradually. Ranging markets oscillate.
  This creates natural momentum patterns in the training data, so the
  momentum model's features actually carry learnable signal.

LIQUIDITY MECHANICS
  8% of candles get a liquidity-sweep wick: a random spike beyond the body
  in one direction (models stop-hunts). 3–4% of candles have micro-gaps
  (open ≠ prior close — models news spikes, overnight gaps). These create
  the wick-based feature variation that makes rejection and pin detection
  meaningful.

3-BAR FUTURE WINDOW WITH PATH METRICS
  All labels are based on what happens over the next 3 H1 bars, not just
  the final close. The labelling engine measures:
  
  MFE   Maximum Favourable Excursion — how far price moved in your favour
  MAE   Maximum Adverse Excursion — how far it moved against you
  Pullback  MFE − final close (did the move hold or retrace?)
  Path range  High-to-low of all 3 future bars / ATR
  
  This is why "confirmed follow-through" means: price moved the right way
  AND stayed there (MFE > pullback × 1.5+). "Partial" means it moved right
  but retraced. This distinction is critical for position sizing in ensemble.

SECTION 3 — MODEL 1: VWAP / VOLUME  (vwap_h1_deploy.py)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FILES
  Deploy  : vwap_h1_deploy.py
  Model   : vwap_h1_best.pth
  Scaler  : vwap_h1_scaler.pkl
  Dataset : synthetic_vwap_h1_dataset.csv  (20,000 samples)

BARS REQUIRED
  115 H1 bars (~5 trading days). Lookback = 100 bars, +15 warmup.

WHAT THIS MODEL SEES
  32 features built around a rolling 50-bar VWAP, volume profile, and
  order-flow delta. Key feature groups:

  VWAP geometry
    vwap_dev_atr        How far close is from VWAP, in ATR units
                        Positive = above VWAP, negative = below
    vwap_sigma          VWAP deviation normalised by VWAP's own std dev
                        |sigma| > 1.5 = price is at an extreme extension
    vwap_slope_z        Rate of change of VWAP over 5 bars, z-scored
                        Positive = VWAP rising (trend), 0 = flat (range)
    vwap_band_walk      Sum of how many bars price stayed above VWAP +1σ
                        High value = sustained bullish VWAP extension
    vwap_dist_pct       % distance of close from VWAP (clipped ±1%)
    vwap_mr_z           Mean-reversion pressure: z-score of |vwap_dev_atr|
                        High = far from VWAP, mean-reversion risk elevated

  VWAP interaction quality
    vwap_reclaim_qual   Quality score of last bullish VWAP cross
                        (body size × volume spike × cross event) clipped [0,3]
    vwap_rejection_qual Quality score of last bearish VWAP cross
    vwap_touch_density  Fraction of last 20 bars that were near VWAP
                        High = choppy/balanced; Low = trending away
    above_vwap_streak   Consecutive bars close has been above VWAP
    cum_delta_vwap_align Sign alignment between delta and VWAP side
                        +1 = delta confirms VWAP position; -1 = divergence

  Volume and order flow
    tick_delta          Proxy for buy/sell pressure: (close−low)/(high−low)×2−1
                        +1 = closed at high (strong bull); −1 = at low (bear)
    cum_delta_20z       20-bar cumulative delta, z-scored over 50 bars
    vol_profile_asym    (up_vol − dn_vol) / total vol, 20-bar window
                        Positive = volume dominated by bullish bars
    buy_press_z         Z-score of rolling buy-volume fraction
    delta_at_vwap       Orderflow imbalance on bars near VWAP
                        High = institutions defending/attacking VWAP
    vol_z30             Volume z-score vs 30-bar mean
    vol_z100            Volume z-score vs 100-bar mean (longer baseline)
    vol_trend_z         Whether volume is trending up or down recently
    session_vol_factor  Actual vol / expected vol for this hour-of-day
                        >1.5 = unusually busy for this time; <0.5 = dead
    vol_at_vwap_z       Volume z-score specifically on near-VWAP bars
    vol_surge_20        Vol z-score clipped to [−3, +3]
    vol_surge_extreme   Vol spike when price is >1.5σ from VWAP
                        Signal of institutional activity at extremes

  Composite signals
    inst_fp             Institutional footprint: 0/1/2/3 score
                        Scores vol spike + near-VWAP + small body (absorption)
    absorption_lvl      Vol × (small body flag): detects hidden absorption
    price_vol_div       Body/ATR ÷ vol_z: high = large move on low volume
                        (divergence — often precedes reversal)
    vol_efficiency      Body per unit of ATR per unit of vol
    vol_mom_div         Sign agreement between price direction and volume trend
                        −1 = price moved but volume disagreed (weak move)

  Structure
    body_ratio          abs_body / range: candle "fill" ratio
    close_grav_f        (close−low) / range: where close sits in the range
    atr14_z             ATR14 z-score vs 50 bars: is volatility high or low?
    vwap_std_feat       VWAP standard deviation (normalised by ATR)
    vol_profile_asym    Repeat — directional volume asymmetry
    cum_delta_vwap_align Orderflow / VWAP side agreement

OUTPUT HEADS

  Head 1: label_next_vwap_dir  (primary trade signal)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "below_away"    Price will move away below VWAP        │
  │ Class 1 → "oscillate_near" Price will stay near / cross VWAP     │
  │ Class 2 → "above_away"    Price will move away above VWAP        │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~27% / 48% / 25%                                   │
  │ Use: Class 0 = bear signal. Class 2 = bull signal.               │
  │       Class 1 = skip this bar, no edge.                           │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 2: label_next_vol_char  (conviction filter)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "declining"   Volume drying up next 3 bars             │
  │ Class 1 → "stable"      Volume normal / unchanged                 │
  │ Class 2 → "surge"       Volume expanding next 3 bars             │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~29% / 43% / 28%                                   │
  │ Use: Surge (2) + directional signal = high confidence entry.      │
  │       Declining (0) + reversal signal = caution / reduce size.    │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 3: label_vwap_interaction  (context / setup type)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "rejection"      Price rejected from VWAP (push down)  │
  │ Class 1 → "test_return"    Price testing VWAP, likely to return   │
  │ Class 2 → "reclaim_hold"   Price crossed VWAP and is holding     │
  │ Class 3 → "walk_extension" Price walking away from VWAP cleanly  │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~30% / 50% / 1% / 19%                              │
  │ Note: reclaim_hold (2) is rare (~1%) but very reliable when       │
  │ it fires — model weight is 8.0× for this class.                   │
  │ Use: Class 3 = trend continuation entry. Class 0 = fade/short.    │
  │       Class 2 = breakout entry with reclaim confirmation.          │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 4: label_next_inst_signal  (who is active)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "distribution"   Institutions likely selling           │
  │ Class 1 → "neutral"        No clear institutional signature       │
  │ Class 2 → "accumulation"   Institutions likely buying            │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~23% / 57% / 20%                                   │
  │ Use: Confirm direction signal. Avoid trading if neutral and        │
  │ directional signal is marginal. Accumulation + bull = strong.     │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 5: label_next_close_location
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "lower_third"   Next candles will close in lower 25%   │
  │ Class 1 → "mid"           Next candles will close in middle 50%  │
  │ Class 2 → "upper_third"   Next candles will close in upper 25%   │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~44% / 12% / 44%  (bimodal — mid is rare)          │
  │ Use: Close location confirms direction. Lower + bear = conviction. │
  │       Upper + bull = conviction. Mid alone = indecision.           │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 6: label_vwap_follow_through  (quality gate)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "no"        Move failed to follow through              │
  │ Class 1 → "partial"   Some follow-through, messy path            │
  │ Class 2 → "confirmed" Clean sustained move in signal direction    │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~20% / 53% / 27%                                   │
  │ Use: Class 2 (confirmed) = full size. Class 1 = half size.        │
  │       Class 0 = do not trade even if direction looks good.         │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TRADE_ACTIVE LOGIC (built-in filter)
  True when: vwap_dir ≠ 1 (oscillate)  AND  follow_through ≥ 1 (partial+)

SECTION 4 — MODEL 2: MOMENTUM  (momentum_h1_deploy.py)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FILES
  Deploy  : momentum_h1_deploy.py
  Model   : momentum_h1_best.pth
  Scaler  : momentum_h1_scaler.pkl
  Dataset : synthetic_momentum_h1_dataset.csv  (25,000 samples)

BARS REQUIRED
  200 H1 bars (~8 trading days). Ichimoku cloud requires 52+26 bar warmup.

WHAT THIS MODEL SEES
  30 features covering displacement, trend quality, ADX, Ichimoku cloud,
  Donchian channels, and multi-timeframe alignment.

  Displacement
    ret_3_atr_norm   3-bar return normalised by ATR/price — ATR-scaled momentum
    ret_3_z          3-bar return z-scored vs 50 bars
    ret_10_atr_norm  10-bar return normalised by ATR/price
    ret_10_z         10-bar return z-scored vs 50 bars
    tension          ret_3_atr_norm × (0.5 − trend_position)
                     Measures how stretched price is against its trend position
                     High positive = overbought relative to recent structure
    momentum_decay   ret_1_norm / (ret_5_norm/5): is 1-bar momentum faster
                     or slower than the 5-bar average momentum?

  Trend quality
    efficiency_ratio  Net displacement / sum of absolute moves over 10 bars
                      1.0 = perfect trend; 0.0 = pure noise/chop
    er_slope          Rate of change of efficiency_ratio over 5 bars
    momentum_persistence  Autocorrelation of 1-bar returns over 30 bars
                          Positive = trending; negative = mean-reverting

  EMA structure
    ema_slope_norm    EMA-20 slope normalised by ATR — trend speed
    ema_acc_norm      EMA slope change (acceleration) normalised by ATR
    slope_alignment   Sign(EMA-20 slope) × Sign(EMA-50 slope)
                      +1 = both EMAs agree direction; −1 = crossed / diverging

  Volatility regime
    atr_z             ATR14 z-score vs 50 bars: is vol high or low?
    range_expansion   3-bar avg range / 20-bar avg range
                      >1.5 = breakout candles; <0.7 = compression
    vol_regime_confirm  atr_z × (range_expansion − 1): vol + range alignment

  Price structure
    trend_position    (close − 20bar_low) / (20bar_high − 20bar_low)
                      0 = at 20-bar low; 1 = at 20-bar high
    break_strength    (close − prev 20-bar high) / ATR
                      Positive = breaking out above prior range
    mean_reversion_stretch  (close − SMA50) / ATR
                            How far price is stretched from 50-bar mean

  ADX (trend strength)
    adx_z             ADX14 z-score vs 50 bars
    adx_slope         Rate of change of ADX — is trend strengthening?
    di_balance        (+DI − −DI) / (+DI + −DI): directional balance
                      +1 = strong bull; −1 = strong bear; 0 = balanced

  Ichimoku
    tk_spread         (Tenkan − Kijun) / ATR: signal line divergence
                      Positive/growing = bullish momentum
    cloud_thickness   |Senkou A − Senkou B| / ATR: cloud width (support/resistance)
    price_vs_cloud    (close − Senkou A) / ATR: position relative to cloud
                      >0 = above cloud (bullish); <0 = inside/below (caution)
    cloud_twist       Sign of (Senkou A − Senkou B): cloud colour
                      +1 = green cloud (bullish); −1 = red cloud (bearish)

  Donchian
    don_position      (close − 20-bar low) / (20-bar range): position in channel
    don_width_z       Donchian width z-score: is channel expanding or contracting?
    don_breakout      (close − prev 20-bar high) / ATR: breakout strength

  Volume
    volume_z          Volume z-score vs 30 bars
    vol_displacement  volume_z × ret_3_z: volume-weighted momentum

OUTPUT HEADS

  Head 1: next_direction  (primary trade signal)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "bearish"    Price moves down over next 3 bars         │
  │ Class 1 → "flat"       Price stays roughly flat                  │
  │ Class 2 → "bullish"    Price moves up over next 3 bars           │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~36% / 31% / 33%  (well-balanced)                  │
  │ Threshold: |net3_move| > 0.56 ATR for directional call           │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 2: next_magnitude  (move size)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "small"    avg body < 0.47 ATR over 3 bars            │
  │ Class 1 → "medium"   avg body 0.47 – 0.83 ATR                   │
  │ Class 2 → "large"    avg body > 0.83 ATR                         │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~33% / 41% / 26%                                   │
  │ Use: Large (2) = widen target. Small (0) = tight range expected.  │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 3: next_regime_behaviour  (market type)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "chop"          All 3 bars choppy, no direction        │
  │ Class 1 → "pullback"      Reversal against prior context          │
  │ Class 2 → "continuation"  Price continues in same direction       │
  │ Class 3 → "breakout"      Strong expansion, large path range      │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~35% / 22% / 24% / 18%                             │
  │ Use: Chop (0) = skip. Continuation (2) = trend entry.            │
  │       Breakout (3) = aggressive entry, wide stop.                  │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 4: next_volatility  (range expectation)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "contracting"   path_range < 1.30 ATR                 │
  │ Class 1 → "normal"        path_range 1.30 – 2.50 ATR            │
  │ Class 2 → "expanding"     path_range > 2.50 ATR                 │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~25% / 45% / 30%                                   │
  │ Use: Expanding (2) = widen stop and target. Contracting (0) =    │
  │       reduce position size, tight scalp targets only.              │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 5: next_structural_bias  (bullish/bearish positioning)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "bearish"   c3 closes in lower 25% + net move down    │
  │ Class 1 → "neutral"   mixed / mid close                          │
  │ Class 2 → "bullish"   c3 closes in upper 25% + net move up      │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~32% / 41% / 27%                                   │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 6: next_momentum_quality  (is momentum accelerating?)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "fading"        b3 < b1×0.50 OR direction flip        │
  │ Class 1 → "stable"        b3 ≈ b1, same direction               │
  │ Class 2 → "accelerating"  b3 > b1×1.25 AND same direction       │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~61% / 19% / 20%                                   │
  │ NOTE: Fading is the most common real-market state. The model      │
  │ is trained to only call "accelerating" when evidence is strong.   │
  │ Use: Accelerating (2) = hold longer, trail stop. Fading (0) =    │
  │       close partial position or skip entry entirely.               │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TRADE_ACTIVE LOGIC
  True when: direction ≠ 1 (flat)  AND  regime ≥ 1 (not chop)
             AND  momentum_quality ≥ 1 (not fading)

SECTION 5 — MODEL 3: MICROSTRUCTURE  (microstructure_h1_deploy.py)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FILES
  Deploy  : microstructure_h1_deploy.py
  Model   : microstructure_h1_best.pth
  Scaler  : microstructure_h1_scaler.pkl
  Dataset : synthetic_microstructure_h1_dataset.csv  (20,000 samples)

BARS REQUIRED
  40 H1 bars (~2 trading days). Shortest lookback of the 5 models.
  Most sensitive to recent candle behaviour.

WHAT THIS MODEL SEES
  32 features focused on single-bar and short-sequence anatomy. No VWAP,
  no Ichimoku, no long rolling windows — pure price action physics.

  Candle anatomy ratios
    body_ratio          abs_body / range  (0=doji, 1=marubozu)
    upper_ratio         upper_wick / range
    lower_ratio         lower_wick / range
    body_ratio_s5       5-bar rolling mean of body_ratio
    upper_ratio_s5      5-bar rolling mean of upper_ratio
    lower_ratio_s5      5-bar rolling mean of lower_ratio
    tail_asym           (upper_wick − lower_wick) / range
                        Positive = upper wick dominates (bearish pressure)
                        Negative = lower wick dominates (bullish pressure)
    tail_to_body        (upper+lower wick) / body: wick-to-body ratio
                        High = rejection / pin bar character

  Candle sizing (z-scored)
    body_z              abs_body z-scored vs 40 bars
    range_z             range z-scored vs 40 bars
    abs_body            Raw absolute body size (price units)
    upper_wick          Raw upper wick size
    lower_wick          Raw lower wick size
    range               Raw bar range

  Order flow
    orderflow_5         Signed volume (up bars positive, down bars negative)
                        summed over 5 bars — directional flow pressure
    orderflow_10        Same over 10 bars
    tick_delta          (close−low)/(range)×2−1: bar-level buy/sell pressure
    cum_delta_5z        5-bar cumulative delta, z-scored vs 20 bars
    signed_vol (→ orderflow)  volume × direction

  Absorption / rejection signals
    absorption_raw      Sum of (small_body × opposite_wick) over 5 bars
                        Detects bars where big volume produces small body
                        = institutional absorption
    absorption_fp       0/1/2/3 composite:
                        +1 if body_ratio < 0.25 (small body)
                        +1 if range > 0.80×ATR10 (wide range)
                        +1 if volume > 1.20×avg10 (above-avg volume)
                        Score of 3 = strong absorption fingerprint
    rejection_qual      dominant_wick / range × vol_ratio × wick_direction
                        Positive = bullish rejection (lower wick dominant)
                        Negative = bearish rejection (upper wick dominant)

  ATR context
    atr5                5-bar average range (short-term ATR proxy)
    atr10               10-bar average range
    pullback_depth      current range / atr5 (is this bar bigger than usual?)
    pullback_z          pullback_depth z-scored vs 20 bars
    exhaustion          (upper+lower wicks) / body: extreme = exhaustion bar

  Physics
    close_gravity       (close−low) / range: 0=at low, 1=at high
    body_mid_disp       Displacement of body midpoint from range midpoint
                        Positive = body skewed toward top (bullish)
    dir_efficiency      body_ratio × (close_gravity if bull else 1−close_gravity)
                        Directional efficiency of the candle

  Volume context
    bar_energy          (range/atr5) × (volume/vol_avg5): energy per bar
                        High = large rangy candle on above-avg volume
    gap_pressure        (open − prev_close) / atr5: gap direction/size

  Interaction
    wick_body_inter     (upper−lower) × body: combined wick asymmetry × size
    body_vol_inter      body × volume: body weighted by participation
    expected_move_raw   atr5 × (1 + |orderflow_5| / avg_vol_5)
    consec_len          Consecutive bars in same direction (streak counter)

OUTPUT HEADS

  Head 1: label_next_flow_dir  (primary directional call)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "bearish"    Net 3-bar move < −0.50 ATR               │
  │ Class 1 → "contested"  Move between −0.50 and +0.50 ATR          │
  │ Class 2 → "bullish"    Net 3-bar move > +0.50 ATR               │
  │                                                                   │
  │ Distribution: ~37% / 29% / 35%                                   │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Head 2: label_next_bar_intent  (what does next bar look like?)
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Class 0 → "absorption"  Small body + dominant wicks              │
  │ Class 1 → "rejection"   Strong asymmetric wick (pin bar type)    │
  │ Class 2 → "impulse"     Large body, closes at extreme            │

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BARS REQUIRED
  135 H1 bars (~5.5 trading days). Needs 120 bars for Bollinger/KC bands.

WHAT THIS MODEL SEES
  32 features built around Bollinger Bands, Keltner Channels, and their
  relationship — the foundation of squeeze / breakout detection.

  Bollinger / Keltner relationship (squeeze core)
    bb_width_z        BB width z-score vs 100 bars
                      Negative = bands extremely narrow (squeeze)
    kc_width_n        KC width normalised by ATR14
    kb_ratio          BB width / KC width
                      <1.0 = BB inside KC = squeeze active
                      >1.0 = BB outside KC = no squeeze
    squeeze_stack     Count of windows (8/14/20) where BB inside KC
                      0 = no squeeze at any scale; 3 = full stack squeeze
    kb_slope          Rate of change of kb_ratio
                      Falling = squeeze deepening; Rising = expanding
    coil_tightness    (1 − kb_ratio) clipped [−1, 1]
                      Positive = tighter squeeze; 0 = no squeeze
    squeeze_maturity  consec_squeeze_bars / rolling_avg_squeeze_length
                      >1.0 = this squeeze is longer than average (overdue)
    vol_potential_e   (1 − hv_pct) × coil_tightness
                      Combined: low historical vol AND tight squeeze
                      High = maximum expansion potential
    exp_velocity      Rate of change of BB width over 3 bars
                      Rising rapidly = expansion beginning

  Donchian bias (lean within squeeze)
    sq_lean           Close position within 20-bar Donchian, during squeeze
                      Positive = leaning bull; Negative = leaning bear
                      Zero outside squeeze (masked by sq_active)
    don_bias          (close − Donchian mid) / Donchian width, always active

  Price position in bands
    bb_position       (close − BB lower) / BB width: position in BB [0–1]
    bb_pos_z          bb_position z-scored vs 50 bars
    kc_position       (close − KC lower) / KC width
    mr_dist           (close − SMA14) / ATR14 clipped [−3, +3]
                      Mean-reversion distance from band midline

  Band walk (trending)
    band_walk_bull    5-bar sum of ATR-normalised closes above upper BB
                      High = price is walking along top band (strong bull)
    band_walk_bear    5-bar sum of ATR-normalised closes below lower BB
                      High = price walking along bottom band (strong bear)

  Breakout confirmation
    bkout_confirm     (above_upper − below_lower) × (vol_z20 + 1)
                      Positive = confirmed bull breakout with volume
                      Negative = confirmed bear breakout with volume
    prebreak_qual     close_grav × body_comp_r × vol_z20
                      Quality score of last bar before breakout fires
    intrabar_fill     body / range: how much of the bar was filled
    hv_percentile     Historical volatility percentile (0–1)
                      Low = recent vol is historically low = squeeze context

  ATR relationships
    atr14_val         Raw ATR14 value
    atr_ratio_z       (ATR5/ATR14) z-scored: is short-term vol diverging?
    atr14             ATR14 (also stored directly)

  Volume
    vol_z20_last      Volume z-score vs 20 bars (most recent)
    vol_z30           Volume z-score vs 30 bars
    vol_z100          Volume z-score vs 100 bars
    vol_trend         Volume trend: (5-bar avg vol) / 20-bar avg vol, z-scored
    vol_sq_div        vol_z20 × (squeeze_stack/3): volume during squeeze
                      High = unusual volume accumulation inside squeeze

  Body
    body_comp_r       body / 20-bar avg body: relative body size
    body_comp_r_z     body_comp_r z-scored vs 50 bars
    close_grav        (close − low) / range: candle close position

SECTION 8 — READING THE PREDICT() OUTPUT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every deploy script returns a Python dict. Structure is identical across
all 5 models:

  result = predictor.predict("EURUSD")

  # Per-head output (one dict per head):
  result["label_next_vwap_dir"] = {
      "class":      2,              # predicted class index
      "label":      "above_away",   # human-readable label
      "confidence": 0.612,          # max softmax probability
      "probs": {
          "below_away":     0.1823,
          "oscillate_near": 0.2057,
          "above_away":     0.6120,  # ← this is the prediction
      }
  }

  # Convenience fields (top-level):
  result["signal"]       = "above_away"   # primary directional label
  result["confidence"]   = 0.612          # confidence in signal
  result["trade_active"] = True/False     # built-in filter

CONFIDENCE INTERPRETATION
  < 0.40   Very uncertain. Softmax barely prefers one class. Do not trade.
  0.40–0.55  Moderate. Use with other model confirmations.
  0.55–0.70  Good. Reasonable to act on if other models agree.
  > 0.70   High. Strong model conviction. Size up.
  > 0.85   Very high. Rare. Often precedes strong moves.

  Note: All 5 models are trained to be confident only when evidence is
  clear. Unlike a random classifier, confidence above 0.70 means the
  feature pattern is unambiguous — not just "more likely than 50%."

PROBABILITY VECTORS FOR SOFT ENSEMBLING
  Do not just use the argmax class. The full "probs" dict is available
  and is the correct input for ensemble logic. See Section 9.

SECTION 9 — ENSEMBLE LOGIC
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The models complement each other. Each answers a different question:

  VWAP       →  Where is price relative to institutional equilibrium?
  Momentum   →  Is the trend strong, regime active, momentum healthy?
  Micro      →  What does the most recent candle structure say?
  Candle     →  What is the narrative of the last 10-bar sequence?
  Breakout   →  Is a squeeze releasing? Will the breakout hold?

STEP 1 — DIRECTIONAL CONSENSUS VOTE
  Each model has a directional head. Assign +1 (bull), −1 (bear), 0 (flat):

  Model            Head                       Bull  Bear  Flat
  ────────────     ─────────────────────      ────  ────  ────
  VWAP             label_next_vwap_dir        2     0     1
  Momentum         next_direction             2     0     1
  Microstructure   label_next_flow_dir        2     0     1
  Candle           label_next_direction       2     0     1
  Breakout         label_next_direction       2     0     1

  Weighted vote:
    score = sum( direction_vote[i] × confidence[i] × model_weight[i] )
  
  Suggested model weights for directional vote:
    VWAP          0.25   (institutional anchor, session-aware)
    Momentum      0.25   (trend regime quality)
    Micro         0.20   (most recent — faster signal, more noise)
    Candle        0.15   (sequence narrative, slower)
    Breakout      0.15   (structural context — not always applicable)

  Score >  0.20 → Long signal
  Score < −0.20 → Short signal
  Score in between → No trade

STEP 2 — QUALITY GATE (must pass before entering)
  Count how many quality heads confirm an actionable setup:

  Head                              Trade OK when
  ──────────────────────────────    ─────────────────────────────────────
  VWAP follow_through               ≥ 1 (partial or confirmed)
  Momentum regime_behaviour         ≥ 1 (not chop)
  Momentum momentum_quality         ≥ 1 (not fading)
  Micro momentum_follow             ≥ 1 (partial or confirmed)
  Candle seq_continuation           ≠ 1 (not indecision)
  Breakout bk_follow_through        ≥ 1 (partial or confirmed)

  Require at least 4 of 6 to be active for a full-size trade.
  3 of 6 = half-size. < 3 = no trade regardless of direction score.

STEP 3 — VOLATILITY SIZING
  Aggregate the volatility heads to set stop distance and target:

  Expanding signals: Momentum.next_volatility=2 OR Breakout.next_volatility=2
    → Use 1.5× ATR stop, 3× ATR target
  Normal signals:
    → Use 1.0× ATR stop, 2× ATR target
  Contracting signals: Both momentum AND breakout show contracting
    → Use 0.7× ATR stop, 1.2× ATR target (scalp mode only)

STEP 4 — SOFT PROBABILITY ENSEMBLE (advanced)
  Instead of voting, average the probability vectors for directional heads:

    bull_prob = mean([
        vwap_probs["above_away"],
        momentum_probs["bullish"],
        micro_probs["bullish"],
        candle_probs["bullish"],
        breakout_probs["bullish"],
    ])
    bear_prob = mean([...])
    flat_prob = mean([...])

  Edge = bull_prob − bear_prob
  Enter long if edge > 0.15 and bull_prob > 0.45
  Enter short if edge < −0.15 and bear_prob > 0.45

STEP 5 — SPECIAL SIGNALS (high-priority overrides)

  VWAP reclaim_hold (class 2, ~1% frequency)
    This is extremely rare and carries 8× model weight. When it fires:
    direction = bull (reclaim) or bear (failed reclaim)
    Treat as high-priority even if other models are mixed.

  Candle pin (class 1, ~2% frequency)
    Pin bar on H1 with high confidence (>0.60) from candle model.
    If microstructure also shows rejection_qual = strong (class 2):
    Counter-trend reversal signal. Size up.

  Momentum accelerating + Breakout confirmed
    Momentum quality = accelerating (2) AND breakout follow = yes (2)
    Both confirming simultaneously: hold trade, trail stop aggressively.

  Momentum fading + Micro bar_intent = absorption
    Exit signal. Fade is deepening AND next bar shows absorption.
    Close or reduce position.

EXAMPLE ENSEMBLE DECISION  (simplified)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  VWAP       → above_away (conf=0.68)   vol=surge    follow=confirmed
  Momentum   → bullish (conf=0.61)      regime=cont  quality=stable
  Micro      → bullish (conf=0.55)      intent=impulse follow=partial
  Candle     → bullish (conf=0.58)      cont=cont    pattern=engulf
  Breakout   → bullish (conf=0.52)      follow=partial vol=expanding
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Direction score = 0.68×0.25 + 0.61×0.25 + 0.55×0.20 + 0.58×0.15 + 0.52×0.15
                  = 0.170 + 0.153 + 0.110 + 0.087 + 0.078 = 0.598  → LONG
  Quality gates: all 5 active (follow=confirmed/partial, regime=cont, quality=stable,
                               follow=partial, cont=continuation, bk=partial)
  Quality score: 5/6 → full size
  Volatility: expanding → 1.5× ATR stop, 3× ATR target
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DECISION: LONG, full size, 1.5 ATR stop, 3 ATR target

SECTION 12 — KNOWN LIMITATIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA
   All 5 models are trained on synthetic data generated by a GARCH + AR(1)
   physics engine. The engine captures volatility clustering, session volume
   profiles, and autocorrelated trends well. However it does not capture:
   - Fundamental news (NFP, FOMC, ECB decisions)
   - Market microstructure changes (central bank intervention)
   - Multi-day correlated regimes (e.g., risk-off periods lasting weeks)
   Models should be used as probabilistic filters, not mechanical signals.

2. LABEL ASYMMETRIES (intentional, not bugs)
   Some labels have intentionally asymmetric class frequencies:
   - Momentum quality: 61% fading (true market reality on H1)
   - Candle seq_continuation: 12% reversal (reversals are genuinely rare)
   - Micro bar_intent: 67% fade (most bars don't produce impulses)
   These are market-realistic distributions. The class weights in training
   compensate, but confidence will naturally be lower for rare classes.

3. NO LOOKAHEAD
   All features are computed strictly from bars [0, t]. No future leakage.
   Feature computation in deploy scripts exactly mirrors the generators.

4. TIMEFRAME LOCK
   All files are calibrated for H1 only. Using M15 or M5 data with these
   models will produce meaningless outputs because the body scales, GARCH
   parameters, and label thresholds are all H1-specific.

5. SCALER DEPENDENCY
   The RobustScaler fitted during training must exactly match the feature
   columns and their order. Do NOT mix scalers and model weights across
   model versions. Always use the _h1_ scaler with the _h1_ model.
