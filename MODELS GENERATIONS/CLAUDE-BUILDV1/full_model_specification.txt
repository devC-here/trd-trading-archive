║  MODEL 1 — MICROSTRUCTURE                                               ║
║  Question: What happened in this bar and how does it fit the last 5?    ║
║  Horizon:  1–5 bars  |  Single-bar auction physics                      ║

PHILOSOPHY:
Each M15 bar is a compressed auction. OHLCV encodes — imperfectly — the
battle between buyers and sellers in those 15 minutes. From it we can
reconstruct: who won the intrabar auction (close gravity), how much energy
was spent vs progress made (efficiency vs absorption), and whether volume
confirms or contradicts the price story (tick delta proxy).

Existing syntheticdata.py has 23 solid base features.
We ADD 9 physics-derived features → 32 total.
The deploy wrapper is rewritten to match exactly.

NEW — PHYSICS-DERIVED (9):

[N1] CLOSE GRAVITY
  formula  = (close - low) / (range + EPS)
  range    = [0, 1]
  The final vote. 1.0 = buyers swept the entire bar.
  DIFFERENT from body_ratio: a doji closing at the high has low body_ratio
  but close_gravity = 1.0 — buyers won despite indecision bar.
  Separates bullish doji from neutral doji — body_ratio cannot do this.

[N2] BODY MIDPOINT DISPLACEMENT
  formula  = ((open + close)/2  -  (high + low)/2) / (range + EPS)
  range    = [-0.5, +0.5]
  Where is the transaction zone (body) relative to geometric bar center?
  +0.4 = body entirely in upper half = buyers claimed most of the bar space.
  Geometrically independent of close_gravity and body_ratio.
  Three features, three different dimensions of the same bar.

[N3] TICK DELTA PROXY
  buy_vol    = close_gravity × volume
  sell_vol   = (1 - close_gravity) × volume
  formula    = (buy_vol - sell_vol) / (volume + EPS)
  range      = [-1, +1]
  Best single-bar directional volume estimate from OHLCV without Level 2.
  Price can go up with delta negative = hidden distribution. Critical.
  This is what prop desk order flow tools do — we approximate it from OHLCV.

[N4] CUMULATIVE DELTA 5-BAR (z-scored)
  formula  = rolling_z(sum(tick_delta × volume, 5 bars), window=20)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  Is directional volume pressure BUILDING over the last 5 bars?
  Positive and rising = institutional accumulation in progress.
  Order flow momentum — distinct from price momentum.

[N5] REJECTION QUALITY SCORE  (signed, volume-weighted)
  dom_wick  = max(upper_wick, lower_wick)
  rej_dir   = +1 if lower_wick > upper_wick else -1
  formula   = (dom_wick / range) × (volume / vol_10_avg) × rej_dir
  range     = [-3, +3]  (clipped)
  Large lower wick on 3× volume = institutional buying defending a level.
  Same wick on 0.3× volume = noise, no one defending.
  Without volume weighting, rejections are ambiguous. With it, unambiguous.

[N6] ABSORPTION FINGERPRINT  (3-component composite)
  comp_a  = (body_ratio < 0.25)              → price went nowhere [0/1]
  comp_b  = (range / atr_10 > 0.80)         → effort was made    [0/1]
  comp_c  = (volume / vol_10_avg > 1.20)    → orders were there  [0/1]
  formula = comp_a + comp_b + comp_c         → [0, 1, 2, 3]
  True iceberg: they try to move price (range), price resists (small body),
  AND large volume cleared. All three together = definitive absorption.
  Single components are noise. The composite is the institutional fingerprint.

[N7] DIRECTIONAL EFFICIENCY
  if bullish: body_ratio × close_gravity
  if bearish: body_ratio × (1 - close_gravity)
  range    = [0, 1]
  Efficient bull bar: large body AND closed near the high.
  Sloppy bull bar: large body but closed in the middle (wick formed above).
  Sloppy bars have significantly higher reversal probability.
  Captures bar QUALITY, not just size or direction.

[N8] BAR ENERGY SIGNATURE
  formula  = (range / atr_5) × (volume / vol_5_avg)
  range    = [0, ~9]  (clipped at 6)
  Range expansion alone can be thin-market noise (no volume).
  Volume expansion alone can be news without direction (tiny range).
  BOTH expanding together = genuine institutional energy event.
  The product is the only reliable single-bar significance filter.

[N9] OPEN GAP PRESSURE
  formula  = (open - prev_close) / (atr_5 + EPS)
  range    = [-3, +3]  (clipped)
  In FX M15 gaps are small but systematic. They encode off-session shifts.
  Positive gap + bullish close = continuation conviction.
  Positive gap + bearish close = gap-fill reversal forming.
  The interaction of gap direction vs close direction is the signal.

HEAD 1 — flow_dominance  [3 classes]
  0 = seller_dominated   1 = contested   2 = buyer_dominated

  PRIMARY FEATURES: tick_delta, close_gravity, cum_delta_5, orderflow_5
  LABEL LOGIC:
    buyer_dominated  : tick_delta > 0.3 AND close_gravity > 0.65 AND cum_delta_5 > 0.5
    seller_dominated : tick_delta < -0.3 AND close_gravity < 0.35 AND cum_delta_5 < -0.5
    contested        : everything else

  WHY THIS FRAMING:
    Old "burst_type" had no grounding. "Flow dominance" maps directly to the
    tick_delta and close_gravity physics we now compute. The model learns
    the combination of intrabar flow + recent order flow context.
    Contested is the most common state — the model must learn to sit still.

HEAD 2 — bar_intent  [4 classes]
  0 = absorption   1 = rejection   2 = impulse   3 = exhaustion

  PRIMARY FEATURES: absorption_fp, rejection_quality, directional_efficiency,
                    bar_energy_signature, tail_to_body, body_z
  LABEL LOGIC:
    absorption  : absorption_fp >= 2  (iceberg fingerprint present)
    rejection   : abs(rejection_quality) > 0.4 AND body_ratio < 0.4
    impulse     : directional_efficiency > 0.5 AND bar_energy > 1.5
    exhaustion  : consec_len >= 4 AND body_ratio < body_ratio_s5 × 0.7
                  AND body_z < 0  (body shrinking in a mature run)
    priority    : absorption > rejection > impulse > exhaustion > default(impulse)

  WHY THIS FRAMING:
    This is the complete vocabulary of candle intent. Every bar is DOING
    one of these four things. The 4-class structure forces the model to
    commit to the mechanism, not just the direction.
    EXHAUSTION is the breakthrough class — no existing candle model detects
    it. A shrinking body in a long run is more predictive of reversal
    than any single wick pattern.

HEAD 3 — cluster_state  [4 classes]
  0 = no_cluster   1 = building   2 = mature   3 = climax

  PRIMARY FEATURES: consec_len, cum_delta_5, orderflow_10, body_ratio_s5,
                    body_z (is body shrinking?), volume trend
  LABEL LOGIC:
    no_cluster  : consec_len <= 1
    building    : consec_len 2-3 AND body_ratio_s5 >= prior_5_avg
    mature      : consec_len 4-6 AND body still healthy
    climax      : consec_len >= 5 AND (body shrinking OR volume rising
                  while body shrinks = classic distribution/climax signature)

  WHY THIS FRAMING:
    The CLIMAX class is the major upgrade over old "cluster_strength".
    A climax run looks strong from the outside (long consecutive run)
    but internally shows distribution (big vol + shrinking body).
    Old model had cluster_strength=3 for BOTH healthy runs and climax runs.
    Completely different trade implications. Now they are separated.

HEAD 4 — rejection_structure  [4 classes]
  0 = none   1 = upper_rejection   2 = lower_rejection   3 = dual_spike

  PRIMARY FEATURES: rejection_quality (signed + volume-weighted),
                    upper_ratio, lower_ratio, close_gravity
  LABEL LOGIC:
    none            : abs(rejection_quality) < 0.15
    upper_rejection : rejection_quality < -0.15 AND upper_ratio > 0.35
    lower_rejection : rejection_quality > +0.15 AND lower_ratio > 0.35
    dual_spike      : upper_ratio > 0.30 AND lower_ratio > 0.30 (both sides)

  WHY THIS FRAMING:
    Now volume-confirmed. A large upper wick on near-zero volume gets
    classified as "none" because no one was defending. Same wick on 2×
    average volume = genuine seller defense = upper_rejection.
    This is a fundamentally more accurate rejection classification.

HEAD 5 — absorption_state  [3 classes]
  0 = none   1 = passive   2 = active

  PRIMARY FEATURES: absorption_fp (3-component), absorption_raw,
                    body_z, range_z, volume / vol_avg
  LABEL LOGIC:
    none    : absorption_fp == 0
    passive : absorption_fp == 1 (one condition met — natural consolidation)
    active  : absorption_fp >= 2 (iceberg-level — multiple conditions firing)

  WHY PASSIVE vs ACTIVE matters:
    Passive = price barely moved, volume normal = just a quiet bar.
    Active = the bar TRIED to move (range real) but was absorbed (body small)
             with elevated volume = someone is defending this level.
    Only ACTIVE absorption is an institutional signal.
    Old "weak/strong" missed this entirely.

HEAD 6 — micro_signal  [3 classes]
  0 = avoid   1 = wait   2 = act

  PRIMARY FEATURES: synthesis of all heads + bar_energy, open_gap_pressure,
                    directional_efficiency
  LABEL LOGIC:
    act  : (flow_dominance != contested) AND (bar_intent == impulse OR rejection)
           AND (cluster_state == building OR mature)
           AND bar_energy > 1.2 AND directional_efficiency > 0.4
    avoid: (bar_intent == absorption) OR (cluster_state == climax)
           OR (bar_energy < 0.5 = thin bar, no energy)
    wait : everything else

  WHY THIS IS THE SYNTHESIS HEAD:
    The context engine reads micro_signal FIRST. It is the microstructure
    model's vote on whether current bar-level conditions support a trade.
    "avoid" is the most important class — it fires when absorption is
    happening (don't fight an iceberg) or when a run is in climax
    (don't join the last desperate buyers).

║  MODEL 2 — CANDLE SEQUENCE                                              ║
║  Question: What STORY are these 10 bars telling as a narrative?         ║
║  Horizon:  10-bar window  |  Sequential auction narrative               ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

PHILOSOPHY:
A sequence of candles is a TIME-SERIES of auction results.
Each bar is one round of the buyer-seller battle.
The sequence tells you who is winning, by how much, and critically —
whether momentum is BUILDING or DECAYING over time.

The breakthrough insight: most pattern models FLAG patterns (engulf=1).
We encode the PHYSICS of how patterns form.
We do not ask "does this look like an engulfing candle?"
We ask "is the force of the latest bar greater than the prior bar,
and is that force in the direction buyers need to take control?"
The engulfing pattern becomes a consequence of that measurement,
not a hard rule.

Second breakthrough: Heikin-Ashi as FEATURES.
HA candles are noise-filtered average-price representations.
We do not use them as a chart. We use their GEOMETRY as features
— the HA body gives a cleaner directional signal than raw candles.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FEATURES  (30 total)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All features computed over a 10-bar rolling window.
"Sequence-level" = computed over the full window.
"Last-bar" = value at the final bar only.

─── GROUP A: LAST-BAR ANATOMY (5 features) ─────────────────────────────

[A1] close_gravity  (last bar)
  = (close - low) / (range + EPS)      range [0, 1]
  The intrabar vote. Same concept as Microstructure but applied to sequences.

[A2] body_ratio  (last bar)
  = abs(close - open) / (range + EPS)  range [0, 1]

[A3] shadow_dominance  (last bar, signed)
  = (lower_wick - upper_wick) / (range + EPS)   range [-1, +1]
  Positive = lower shadow dominates = buyers absorbing sells below.
  Negative = upper shadow dominates = sellers absorbing buys above.
  Signed and range-normalized — different from tail_asym.

[A4] vol_weight  (last bar, relative)
  = volume / volume.rolling(20).mean()   range [0, ~4] clipped at 3
  Is this bar above or below its historical average participation?

[A5] directional_efficiency  (last bar)
  = body_ratio × close_gravity         if bullish
  = body_ratio × (1 - close_gravity)   if bearish
  Range [0, 1]. Same physics as Microstructure N7.

─── GROUP B: SEQUENCE NARRATIVE PHYSICS (8 features) ───────────────────

[B1] close_gravity_slope  (OLS slope over 10 bars, normalized)
  = OLS_slope(close_gravity_10_bars) / (std(close_gravity_10_bars) + EPS)
  Are buyers PROGRESSIVELY winning intrabar? Rising slope = accumulation.
  This is the candle equivalent of Efficiency Ratio.
  The single most powerful sequence feature — no single-bar model has it.

[B2] body_entropy  (information-theoretic measure)
  = std(body_ratio, 10) / (mean(body_ratio, 10) + EPS)
  Low entropy = consistent bar sizes = controlled institutional movement.
  High entropy = erratic = noise, disagreement.
  A trending sequence has LOW entropy. Pre-reversal sequences spike HIGH.
  Derived from information theory applied to market physics.

[B3] wick_pressure_slope  (OLS slope over 10 bars)
  = OLS_slope(shadow_dominance_10_bars) / (std(shadow_dominance) + EPS)
  Is wick pressure building in one direction across bars?
  Rising wick_slope = buyers absorbing more seller pressure each bar.
  This is HOW institutional accumulation looks in candle physics over time.

[B4] volume_narrative  (directional volume conviction trend)
  bar_conviction = sign(close - open) × body_ratio × (volume / vol_20_avg)
  volume_narrative = OLS_slope(bar_conviction_10_bars)   then z-scored
  Is directional conviction GROWING across the sequence?
  Rising = institutions accumulating consistently.
  Falling = distribution — smart money exiting as retail buys.
  The most powerful institutional intent detector available from OHLCV.

[B5] sequence_close_harmony  (correlation-based coherence)
  = Pearson_correlation(close_gravity_10, range_10)
  Do energetic bars (large range) ALSO have directional resolution?
  harmony > 0.5 = large bars are directional = clean trend.
  harmony < 0 = large bars are reversal bars = conflict, chop.
  Captures structural coherence no single-bar feature can see.

[B6] momentum_handoff  (direction shift mid-sequence)
  early_dir = sign(sum(close - open, bars 1-5))
  late_dir  = sign(sum(close - open, bars 6-10))
  handoff   = 1 if early_dir != late_dir else 0   [binary: did direction change?]
  handoff_str = (late_5_body_avg - early_5_body_avg) / (atr_14 + EPS)
  handoff detects reversals forming BEFORE they complete.
  handoff_str says whether the new direction has more energy than the old.

[B7] sequence_acceleration  (late half vs early half body size)
  = (mean(body_ratio, bars 6-10) - mean(body_ratio, bars 1-5)) / (mean_full + EPS)
  Positive = sequence getting more decisive = trend accelerating.
  Negative = sequence getting less decisive = trend decelerating.
  The most important timing feature — join if accelerating, fade if decelerating.

[B8] vol_climax_divergence  (body per unit of volume)
  vol_surge_10 = volume.rolling(10).mean() / volume.rolling(30).mean()
  body_per_vol = mean(body_ratio, 10) / (vol_surge_10 + EPS)
  Falling body_per_vol (volume rising faster than bodies) = distribution.
  Rising body_per_vol = efficient, clean directional bars.
  This is the classic "churning" detection used by institutional desks.

─── GROUP C: PATTERN PHYSICS as CONTINUOUS SCORES (7 features) ─────────

[C1] engulf_quality  (continuous, signed — replaces binary flag)
  = (abs_body_last - abs_body_prior) / (atr_14 + EPS) × sign(close - open)
  Positive = bullish engulf quality. Negative = bearish engulf quality.
  A tiny candle engulfing a doji = near-zero score.
  Large candle engulfing large prior bar = high score = real conviction flip.
  The DEGREE of engulfing encodes the FORCE. Binary flags lose this entirely.

[C2] inside_compression_energy
  consec_inside = consecutive bars: high < prev_high AND low > prev_low
  mother_range  = range at start of inside sequence / atr_14
  = consec_inside × mother_range
  Energy coiling: each inside bar is a compressed spring.
  Larger mother bar × more inside bars = more explosive the eventual move.
  Encodes BOTH probability AND expected magnitude in one number.

[C3] ha_body  (Heikin-Ashi denoised direction, last bar)
  ha_close = (O + H + L + C) / 4
  ha_open  = EWM(ha_close, span=3)
  ha_body  = (ha_close - ha_open) / (atr_14 + EPS)
  HA filters noise using average prices. The HA body gives the cleaner
  underlying directional signal. HA body < 0 = denoised bearish.
  This is the first time HA is used as a FEATURE (not a chart type).

[C4] ha_body_slope  (is HA direction accelerating?)
  = (ha_body - ha_body.shift(3)) / (atr_14 + EPS)
  Rising ha_body_slope = trend accelerating in denoised space.
  Crossing zero = trend change confirmed even before raw candles show it.

[C5] pin_bull_quality  (continuous)
  = (lower_wick / range) × (1 if body_ratio < 0.35 else 0.5) × close_gravity
  A perfect bullish pin: wick > 60% range, small body, close near high.
  Score captures the DEGREE of pinness and close confirmation.

[C6] pin_bear_quality  (continuous)
  = (upper_wick / range) × (1 if body_ratio < 0.35 else 0.5) × (1 - close_gravity)

[C7] doji_context  (doji meaning depends on position in sequence)
  doji_score = max(0, 0.15 - body_ratio) / 0.15   → 1 if body < 5% range
  seq_position = (close - min(close, 10)) / (max(close, 10) - min(close, 10) + EPS)
  doji_context = doji_score × (seq_position - 0.5)
  Positive = doji at TOP of 10-bar range (after bull run) = bearish doji.
  Negative = doji at BOTTOM (after bear run) = bullish doji.
  Context changes meaning entirely. Most models ignore this.

─── GROUP D: RELATIVE SIZING (3 features) ───────────────────────────────

[D1] body_vs_avg   = abs_body / abs_body.rolling(30).mean()   [clipped 0-4]
[D2] range_vs_avg  = range / range.rolling(30).mean()         [clipped 0-4]
[D3] body_accel    = body_ratio - body_ratio.rolling(5).mean() [delta]
  Is this bar more or less decisive than its recent baseline?

─── GROUP E: FRACTAL PHYSICS (2 features) ───────────────────────────────

[E1] fractal_bull
  = 1 if body_ratio_1 > mean(body_ratio_3) > mean(body_ratio_10)
         AND close[-1] > close[-10]  (directional confirmation)
  else 0
  Acceleration at EVERY timescale = the multi-scale self-similarity signature
  of a genuine momentum breakout. False breakouts lack this structure.

[E2] fractal_bear
  = 1 if same condition but close[-1] < close[-10]

  WHY: Conviction is orthogonal to direction. High conviction bearish sequence

  trust the direction. Low conviction = wait regardless of direction.

HEAD 4 — wick_pressure  [3 classes]
  0 = upper_pressure   1 = balanced   2 = lower_pressure
  PRIMARY FEATURES: wick_pressure_slope, shadow_dominance (last bar),
                    mean(shadow_dominance, 10), pin_bull/bear quality
  LABEL LOGIC:
    lower_pressure : wick_slope > 0.2 AND mean_shadow > 0.1  (buyers absorbing)
    upper_pressure : wick_slope < -0.2 AND mean_shadow < -0.1  (sellers absorbing)
    balanced       : everything in between
  WHY: Wick pressure measures the DEFENSIVE force at one side of the market.
  Progressive lower_pressure = buyers building a floor. Structural bullish.
  Not about a single candle — about the 10-bar trend of wick pressure.

HEAD 5 — sequence_phase  [4 classes]
  0 = coiling   1 = ignition   2 = trending   3 = exhausting
  PRIMARY FEATURES: inside_compression_energy, fractal_bull/bear, ha_body_slope,
                    sequence_acceleration, vol_climax_divergence, body_entropy
  LABEL LOGIC:
    coiling    : inside_compression_energy > 1.0 OR (body_entropy > 0.7 AND range_vs_avg < 0.8)
    ignition   : fractal_bull OR fractal_bear — multi-scale acceleration just fired
    trending   : ha_body_slope > 0 AND seq_acceleration > 0 AND body_entropy < 0.5
    exhausting : seq_acceleration < -0.1 AND consec_len > 4
                 OR vol_climax_divergence < 0.4 AND vol_surge > 1.5
  WHY THIS IS HEAD 5:
    The phase tells you WHERE in the candle narrative cycle you are.
    Coiling = spring compressing, wait for ignition.
    Ignition = fractal acceleration just fired, momentum entry.
    Trending = healthy mature trend, continuation entries OK.
    Exhausting = body shrinking, volume churning, do not join.
    This single head replaces the need for most of the old model's logic.

HEAD 6 — candle_signal  [3 classes]
  0 = avoid   1 = wait   2 = act
  PRIMARY FEATURES: synthesis of all above
  LABEL LOGIC:
    act  : pattern_type in (impulse_bull/bear OR pin_bull/bear)
           AND sequence_dominance aligns with pattern direction
           AND conviction_level >= medium
           AND sequence_phase in (ignition, trending)
           AND body_vs_avg > 1.1
    avoid: sequence_phase == exhausting
           OR conviction_level == low AND body_entropy > 0.8
           OR pattern_type == indecision AND no momentum context
    wait : everything else
  WHY: Same synthesis-gate philosophy as Microstructure.
  Context engine reads candle_signal first. The individual heads provide
  the justification that the context engine can inspect.

║  MODEL 3 — BREAKOUT                                                     ║
║  Question: Is the spring coiling or releasing? Quality of the move?     ║
║  Horizon:  14–100 bars  |  Compression → expansion cycle physics        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

PHILOSOPHY:
Volatility is mean-reverting. This is a mathematical law.
Low volatility is ALWAYS followed by higher volatility.
The question is only: HOW compressed is the spring, HOW LONG has
it been compressing, and WHERE will it release?

Standard Bollinger Bands measure ONE dimension of this (std dev compression).
Keltner Channels measure a DIFFERENT dimension (ATR compression).
The TTM Squeeze — BB inside KC — is the intersection.
But even TTM Squeeze only measures WHETHER a squeeze exists, not
its maturity, its historical extremeness, its directional lean,
or the quality of the candle at the breakout moment.

We measure ALL dimensions simultaneously.
The result is a breakout physics engine, not just a squeeze detector.

KEY INNOVATIONS vs existing breakoutdeploy.py:
  1. No ta library dependency — everything native numpy/pandas
  2. Multi-period squeeze stack (8, 14, 20 period confirmation)
  3. Squeeze maturity index (time dimension)
  4. Volatility potential energy (historical extremeness × current compression)
  5. Directional lean inside squeeze (predict direction BEFORE breakout)
  6. ATR ratio z-score (fast/slow ATR divergence as regime transition detector)

FEATURES  (32 total)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

─── GROUP A: SQUEEZE DETECTION — MULTI-DIMENSIONAL (8 features) ─────────

[A1] bb_width_z  (how compressed is BB relative to its history?)
  BB_std_20    = close.rolling(20).std()
  BB_width     = 4 × BB_std_20                      [upper - lower = 2×2σ]
  formula      = rolling_z(BB_width, 100)
  range        = [-3, +3]
  Negative = currently more compressed than historical average.
  -2.0 = historically extreme compression (bottom 2.3% of 100-bar history).

[A2] kc_width  (Keltner channel width, ATR-normalized)
  KC_upper = EMA(close, 20) + 1.5 × ATR(10)
  KC_lower = EMA(close, 20) - 1.5 × ATR(10)
  kc_width = (KC_upper - KC_lower) / (ATR(14) + EPS)
  range    = [1, 6]  (usually around 3)

[A3] squeeze_stack  (multi-period TTM Squeeze — novel)
  BB_8_inside_KC_8   = (4×std(close,8))  < (3×ATR(8))   → [0/1]
  BB_14_inside_KC_14 = (4×std(close,14)) < (3×ATR(14))  → [0/1]
  BB_20_inside_KC_20 = (4×std(close,20)) < (3×ATR(20))  → [0/1]
  formula  = sum of the three                            → [0, 1, 2, 3]
  WHY NOVEL: Standard TTM uses only one period.
  stack == 3 means ALL three timeframes are squeezed simultaneously.
  This occurs rarely and precedes the most explosive moves in FX.

[A4] kb_ratio  (BB width / KC width — the squeeze ratio)
  formula  = BB_width_14 / (KC_width_14 × ATR(14) + EPS)
  range    = [0.3, 2.5]
  < 1.0 = BB inside KC = squeeze active. Lower = tighter squeeze.

[A5] kb_slope  (rate of change of squeeze ratio — the TRIGGER detector)
  formula  = OLS_slope(kb_ratio, 10_bars) normalized by std(kb_ratio, 50)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  CRITICAL FEATURE: When kb_slope turns POSITIVE after a squeeze period,
  the BB is expanding relative to KC = spring releasing.
  This detects the EXACT inflection point of breakout initiation.
  No existing indicator captures this transition. It is the trigger.

[A6] squeeze_maturity  (time pressure — how overdue is this squeeze?)
  squeeze_active     = (kb_ratio < 1.0).astype(int)
  consec_squeeze     = consecutive bars where squeeze_active == 1
  avg_squeeze_len    = rolling mean of past squeeze durations (100-bar lookback)
  formula            = consec_squeeze / (avg_squeeze_len + EPS)
  range              = [0, ~4]
  > 1.0 = squeeze older than historical average.
  > 2.0 = OVERDUE. The longer beyond average, the more explosive release.
  Time is itself a dimension of compression energy.

[A7] vol_potential_energy  (physics: stored energy = compression × historical extremeness)
  hv_pct        = ATR(14).rolling(100).rank() / 100.0   [0=lowest, 1=highest vol]
  bb_compression = max(0, 1 - BB_width / BB_width.rolling(50).mean())
  formula        = (1 - hv_pct) × bb_compression
  range          = [0, 1]
  High = historically rare low volatility + current compression above average.
  This is the STORED ENERGY of the spring. The kinetic energy when released
  will be proportional to this stored potential energy.

[A8] squeeze_directional_lean  (predict direction BEFORE breakout fires)
  don_mid_20    = (don_high_20 + don_low_20) / 2
  lean_raw      = (close - don_mid_20) / (don_width_20 / 2 + EPS)
  formula       = EWM(lean_raw, span=5) × (squeeze_active)
                  [= 0 when no squeeze, = lean when squeeze active]
  range         = [-1, +1]
  WHERE is price leaning inside the compression range?
  Consistently positive during squeeze = bullish breakout more likely.
  This is the only feature that can predict breakout direction BEFORE it occurs.

─── GROUP B: EXPANSION MEASUREMENT (5 features) ─────────────────────────

[B1] atr_ratio_z  (fast ATR vs slow ATR — regime transition signal)
  atr_fast  = ATR(5)
  atr_slow  = ATR(20)
  atr_ratio = atr_fast / (atr_slow + EPS)
  formula   = rolling_z(atr_ratio, 50)
  range     = [-3, +3]
  Negative = recent bars quiet vs longer-term average = coiling.
  Positive = recent bars explosive vs longer-term = expansion in progress.
  Better than raw ATR z-score: it is self-referencing, not price-scale dependent.

[B2] expansion_velocity  (how FAST is volatility expanding?)
  atr_3 = ATR(3)
  formula = (atr_3 - atr_3.shift(5)) / (atr_3.shift(5) + EPS)
  range   = [-0.5, +1.5]  clipped
  Slow expansion (< 0.2) = controlled institutional breakout = more reliable.
  Explosive (> 0.5) = news spike, thin-air move = higher reversal risk.
  Same breakout, completely different trade quality.

[B3] band_walk_bull  (cumulative area above BB upper — not just count)
  formula  = sum(max(0, close - BB_upper_20), last 10 bars) / (ATR(14) + EPS)
  range    = [0, ~5]
  A bar 0.1 ATR above the band ≠ a bar 2 ATR above.
  The AREA captures cumulative force of the band walk.
  Large value = price powered through band consistently = real breakout.

[B4] band_walk_bear  (same, below BB lower)
  formula  = sum(max(0, BB_lower_20 - close), last 10 bars) / (ATR(14) + EPS)

[B5] hv_percentile  (historical volatility context)
  log_ret   = log(close / close.shift(1))
  hv_14     = log_ret.rolling(14).std() × sqrt(96)   [96 bars/day on M15]
  formula   = hv_14.rolling(100).rank() / 100.0
  range     = [0, 1]
  Is current volatility historically low or high?
  0.05 = only 5% of last 100 bars had lower vol = extreme compression.

─── GROUP C: POSITION WITHIN BANDS (5 features) ─────────────────────────

[C1] bb_position  (where is price within BB?)
  formula  = (close - BB_lower_20) / (BB_width_20 + EPS)
  range    = [0, 1]  (can exceed briefly)

[C2] bb_position_z  (is current position unusual within BB context?)
  formula  = rolling_z(bb_position, 30)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  A bb_position of 0.95 during a squeeze means something different than 0.95
  during normal volatility. Z-score contextualizes the position.

[C3] kc_position  (where is price within Keltner channel?)
  formula  = (close - KC_lower) / (KC_upper - KC_lower + EPS)
  range    = [0, 1]
  KC uses ATR (actual volatility). Difference between bb_position and kc_position
  encodes deviation of price-based vol from ATR-based vol = novel divergence signal.

[C4] mean_reversion_dist  (how far from BB midline, band-width normalized)
  formula  = (close - EMA(close, 20)) / (BB_width_20 / 2 + EPS)
  range    = [-1.5, +1.5]  clipped at ±2
  +1.0 = at upper band. -1.0 = at lower. 0 = at midline.
  Auto-scales with volatility — more informative than raw distance.

[C5] coil_tightness  (short-term price compression vs medium-term)
  formula  = rolling_z(close.rolling(5).std() / close.rolling(30).std(), 100)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  Indicator-free compression detection using pure price statistics.
  Works even when BB/KC calculations are noisy.

─── GROUP D: CANDLE-LEVEL BREAKOUT QUALITY (6 features) ─────────────────

[D1] body_compression_ratio  (candle-level squeeze analog)
  formula  = body_ratio.rolling(5).mean() / body_ratio.rolling(30).mean()
  range    = [0.2, 3.0]
  Candle bodies systematically smaller than historical = micro-squeeze.
  Ratio spikes > 1.5 = body explosion = breakout candles forming.
  This is ORTHOGONAL to BB/KC — uses candle anatomy, not price range.

[D2] prebreakout_candle_quality  (composite breakout candle score)
  close_grav = (close - low) / (range + EPS)
  dir_close  = close_grav if bullish else (1 - close_grav)
  vol_surge  = min(volume / vol_20_avg, 3.0)
  formula    = body_ratio × dir_close × vol_surge
  range      = [0, ~3] normalized to [0, 1]
  The initiating breakout candle should have large body, close at extreme,
  AND elevated volume. All three together = genuine breakout initiation.

[D3] intrabar_fill  (directional fill of the bar)
  formula  = (close - open) / (range + EPS)   signed [-1, +1]
  What fraction of the bar's range was used for directional progress?
  +1 = opened at low, closed at high = maximum directional fill.

[D4] vol_squeeze_divergence  (hidden accumulation inside squeeze)
  formula  = vol_z_20 × squeeze_active
  Positive = elevated volume DURING a squeeze = institutional accumulation.
  Negative / zero = standard quiet consolidation.
  High vol inside a squeeze is the accumulation signal before the explosion.

[D5] breakout_confirmation  (vol × candle quality in breakout direction)
  formula  = vol_z_30 × prebreakout_candle_quality × sign(close - EMA(close, 20))
  range    = [-3, +3]
  Volume confirming a breakout in the direction of the move.
  Negative = volume on the wrong side = false breakout / trap.

[D6] donchian_bias  (where within Donchian range is price?)
  don_high_20  = high.rolling(20).max()
  don_low_20   = low.rolling(20).min()
  formula      = (close - don_low_20) / (don_high_20 - don_low_20 + EPS)
  range        = [0, 1]
  Different from bb_position because Donchian uses actual high/low extremes,
  not standard deviation. More relevant for breakout detection.

─── GROUP E: VOLUME CONTEXT (3 features) ────────────────────────────────

[E1] vol_z_30    = rolling_z(volume, 30)       range [-3, +3]
[E2] vol_surge   = volume / vol_20_avg          range [0.1, 5] clipped at 4
[E3] vol_trend   = OLS_slope(volume, 10) / (vol_30_std + EPS)   range [-3, +3]

OUTPUT HEADS  (6 heads)

HEAD 1 — squeeze_state  [4 classes]
  0 = no_squeeze   1 = forming   2 = active   3 = overdue
  PRIMARY FEATURES: squeeze_stack, kb_ratio, squeeze_maturity, vol_potential_energy
  LABEL LOGIC:
    no_squeeze : squeeze_stack == 0
    forming    : squeeze_stack == 1 OR (kb_ratio falling but > 1.0)
    active     : squeeze_stack >= 2 AND squeeze_maturity < 1.5
    overdue    : squeeze_stack >= 2 AND squeeze_maturity >= 1.5
                 OR vol_potential_energy > 0.5
  WHY 4 not 3: OVERDUE is a fundamentally different state than ACTIVE.
  An overdue squeeze has higher explosive potential AND higher urgency.
  Treating it as "just active" loses the time-pressure information.

HEAD 2 — breakout_direction  [3 classes]
  0 = bearish   1 = neutral   2 = bullish
  PRIMARY FEATURES: squeeze_directional_lean, donchian_bias,
                    intrabar_fill, band_walk_bull/bear, mean_reversion_dist
  LABEL LOGIC:
    bullish : (squeeze_lean > 0.2 AND donchian_bias > 0.6)
              OR (band_walk_bull > 0.3 AND intrabar_fill > 0.3)
    bearish : (squeeze_lean < -0.2 AND donchian_bias < 0.4)
              OR (band_walk_bear > 0.3 AND intrabar_fill < -0.3)
    neutral : insufficient directional evidence
  WHY: This head attempts to predict direction BEFORE the breakout completes.
  squeeze_directional_lean is the key — it reads the lean inside the coil.

HEAD 3 — breakout_quality  [4 classes]
  0 = no_breakout   1 = false_break   2 = developing   3 = confirmed
  PRIMARY FEATURES: prebreakout_candle_quality, breakout_confirmation,
                    vol_surge, band_walk, kb_slope (releasing = confirmed)
  LABEL LOGIC:
    no_breakout  : squeeze_active OR atr_ratio_z < 0 (still compressing)
    false_break  : close briefly outside BB but: vol low OR candle quality poor
                   OR price re-enters band within 2 bars
    developing   : kb_slope turning positive, candle quality moderate
    confirmed    : kb_slope > 0.3 AND candle_quality > 0.4 AND vol_surge > 1.3
                   AND price closed outside band AND stayed there
  WHY 4 classes: FALSE_BREAK is the most valuable class — no existing model
  reliably detects false breakouts. The combination of kb_slope + vol + candle
  quality distinguishes real vs false with high precision.

HEAD 4 — volatility_phase  [4 classes]
  0 = deeply_compressing   1 = compressing   2 = transitioning   3 = expanding
  PRIMARY FEATURES: bb_width_z, atr_ratio_z, expansion_velocity,
                    hv_percentile, coil_tightness
  LABEL LOGIC:
    deeply_compressing : bb_width_z < -1.5 AND hv_percentile < 0.15
    compressing        : bb_width_z < -0.5 AND atr_ratio_z < 0
    transitioning      : kb_slope turning positive (expansion beginning)
                         OR atr_ratio_z crossing 0
    expanding          : bb_width_z > 0 AND atr_ratio_z > 0.5 AND expansion_velocity > 0.1
  WHY: Volatility regime has four distinct states, not two.
  DEEPLY_COMPRESSING is rare and precedes the most violent moves.
  TRANSITIONING is the tradeable moment — vol just started expanding.

HEAD 5 — band_structure  [3 classes]
  0 = lower_walk   1 = mid_range   2 = upper_walk
  PRIMARY FEATURES: band_walk_bull, band_walk_bear, bb_position_z,
                    kc_position, mean_reversion_dist
  LABEL LOGIC:
    upper_walk : band_walk_bull > 0.5 OR (bb_position > 0.85 consistently)
    lower_walk : band_walk_bear > 0.5 OR (bb_position < 0.15 consistently)
    mid_range  : neither walk condition met, price oscillating near midline
  WHY: Band walking = sustained institutional directional pressure.
  A band walk with a squeeze = the most powerful combination in breakout trading.

HEAD 6 — entry_timing  [3 classes]
  0 = avoid   1 = wait   2 = now
  PRIMARY FEATURES: synthesis of all above
  LABEL LOGIC:
    now  : squeeze_state in (active, overdue)
           AND breakout_direction != neutral (lean is clear)
           AND breakout_quality in (developing, confirmed)
           AND volatility_phase in (transitioning, expanding)
           AND vol_squeeze_divergence > 0.3 (accumulation confirmed)
    avoid: no_squeeze AND mid_range (nothing happening)
           OR false_break detected
           OR deeply_compressing with no lean (direction unknown, could explode either way)
    wait : everything else — squeeze forming but not ready, or direction unclear

PHILOSOPHY:
Volume is the only feature that reveals PARTICIPANT BEHAVIOR, not just price.
Price can be manipulated short-term. Volume cannot be faked —
every tick represents a real order that cleared the market.
In FX we only have tick volume (price changes per bar), not real volume.
But tick volume correlates highly with real volume and carries the same
behavioral information.

CRITICAL DESIGN DECISION — ROLLING VWAP vs SESSION VWAP:
Session VWAP resets at midnight UTC. This is fragile for FX because:
  1. FX has no real market open/close — it is 24-hour
  2. Midnight UTC is not a meaningful financial event
  3. Session reset creates discontinuities that confuse the model
We use ROLLING 50-BAR VWAP (= ~12.5 hours on M15).
This always reflects the most recent 12.5 hours of fair value.
Consistent, stable, always valid.

KEY INNOVATIONS vs existing VWdeploy.py:
  1. VWAP sigma bands (volume-weighted std dev — more informed than BB)
  2. Tick delta proxy accumulated over 20 bars (cumulative delta)
  3. Volume profile asymmetry (bull vol vs bear vol over 20 bars)
  4. Institutional footprint score (3-component iceberg detector at VWAP)
  5. Session-adjusted volume (removes time-of-day bias for FX)
  6. VWAP reclaim/rejection quality (volume-confirmed crossing)
  7. Eliminated duplicate feature (vwap_x_volume == vwap_vol_displacement was identical)

─── GROUP A: VWAP CONSTRUCTION (6 features) ─────────────────────────────

[A1] vwap_dev_atr  (distance from fair value in ATR units)
  typical_price = (high + low + close) / 3
  cum_tp_vol    = (typical_price × volume).rolling(50).sum()
  cum_vol       = volume.rolling(50).sum()
  vwap_50       = cum_tp_vol / (cum_vol + EPS)
  formula       = (close - vwap_50) / (ATR(14) + EPS)
  range         = [-4, +4]  clipped
  Core fair value distance. +1.0 = price is 1 ATR above 50-bar fair value.
  The fundamental VWAP feature — everything else is built around this.

[A2] vwap_sigma  (VWAP standard deviation — volume-weighted)
  vwap_var = rolling_50: Σ(volume × (typical_price - vwap)²) / Σ(volume)
  vwap_std = sqrt(vwap_var) / (ATR(14) + EPS)   [ATR-normalized]
  formula  = vwap_dev_atr / (vwap_std + EPS)    [how many σ from VWAP]
  range    = [-3, +3]
  This is a VOLUME-WEIGHTED version of Bollinger Bands.
  More informed than BB because it weights by where transactions occurred.
  +2σ = 2 volume-weighted standard deviations above fair value = fade zone.
  Institutional desks use VWAP±1σ/2σ as primary order execution reference levels.

[A3] vwap_slope_z  (is fair value rising or falling?)
  formula  = rolling_z(vwap_50.diff(5) / (ATR(14) + EPS), window=30)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  Rising VWAP = cumulative average transaction price moving up = net buying.
  The slope of VWAP is among the cleanest fair-value trend signals available.

[A4] vwap_reclaim_quality  (volume-confirmed cross above VWAP)
  cross_bull    = (close > vwap_50) & (close.shift(1) < vwap_50.shift(1))
  cross_quality = abs(close - vwap_50) / (ATR(14) + EPS) × vol_z_30
  formula       = cross_quality × cross_bull   [= 0 most bars]
  range         = [0, ~3]
  Not all VWAP crosses matter. A strong close above VWAP + elevated volume
  = genuine institutional reclaim of fair value. Weak cross = noise.

[A5] vwap_rejection_quality  (volume-confirmed cross below VWAP)
  cross_bear    = (close < vwap_50) & (close.shift(1) > vwap_50.shift(1))
  formula       = cross_quality × cross_bear   [signed negative]
  range         = [0, ~3]

[A6] vwap_touch_density  (how often has price returned to VWAP recently?)
  touch    = abs(close - vwap_50) < (0.15 × ATR(14))
  formula  = touch.rolling(20).sum()   → [0, 20]
  range    = [0, 10]  typically
  High density = VWAP is acting as a strong reference level (respected).
  Low density = price has been away from VWAP = stretched, potential for mean reversion.

─── GROUP B: TICK DELTA — DIRECTIONAL VOLUME RECONSTRUCTION (5 features) ─

[B1] tick_delta_bar  (single-bar directional volume proxy)
  formula  = ((close - low) / (range + EPS)) × 2 - 1   → [-1, +1]
  The intrabar net flow proxy. Same physics as Microstructure N3 but
  used here for ACCUMULATION over time rather than single-bar flow.

[B2] cum_delta_20_z  (20-bar cumulative directional volume, z-scored)
  delta_vol     = tick_delta_bar × volume
  cum_delta_20  = delta_vol.rolling(20).sum()
  formula       = rolling_z(cum_delta_20, 50)
  range         = [-3, +3]
  Is net buying pressure BUILDING over the last 20 bars?
  Diverges from price when distribution is occurring.
  This is the institutional accumulation detector.

[B3] vol_profile_asymmetry  (bull vol vs bear vol ratio over 20 bars)
  up_vol   = volume.where(close > open, 0).rolling(20).sum()
  dn_vol   = volume.where(close < open, 0).rolling(20).sum()
  formula  = (up_vol - dn_vol) / (up_vol + dn_vol + EPS)
  range    = [-1, +1]
  +1 = ALL recent volume happened on bullish bars = buyers in complete control.
  This is different from cum_delta: uses bar-level direction (open vs close),
  not intrabar gravity (low vs close). Independent measurement.

[B4] buying_pressure_ratio_z  (rolling fraction of volume on up-bars)
  bp_ratio = vol.where(close > open, 0).rolling(20).sum() / (vol.rolling(20).sum() + EPS)
  formula  = rolling_z(bp_ratio, 50)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  Z-scored so it detects UNUSUAL buying dominance vs that instrument's baseline.
  Better than raw OBV because self-normalizing and stationary.

[B5] delta_at_vwap  (directional pressure specifically near VWAP)
  near_vwap = abs(close - vwap_50) < (0.3 × ATR(14))
  formula   = rolling_z(delta_vol.where(near_vwap, 0).rolling(10).sum(), 30)
  range     = [-3, +3]
  Delta measured ONLY when price is near VWAP = directional pressure at fair value.
  This is the highest-quality volume signal — flow at the contested level.

─── GROUP C: VOLUME CHARACTER (6 features) ──────────────────────────────

[C1] vol_z_30   = rolling_z(volume, 30)    range [-3, +3]

[C2] vol_z_100  = rolling_z(volume, 100)   range [-3, +3]
  Long-term context — is this bar unusual vs the last 100 bars?
  Different from vol_z_30 in slow-building surges.

[C3] vol_trend  (is participation increasing or decreasing?)
  formula  = OLS_slope(volume, 10) / (volume.rolling(30).std() + EPS)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  Rising vol in the direction of the trend = healthy continuation.
  Falling vol during trend = distribution / quiet exhaustion.

[C4] session_vol_factor  (time-of-day adjusted volume — FX specific)
  bar_hour     = hour from timestamp (0-23 UTC)
  expected_vol = rolling_20_day_hourly_avg[bar_hour]   [avg vol for this hour]
  formula      = volume / (expected_vol + EPS)
  range        = [0.1, 5]
  CRITICAL FOR FX: A vol z-score of +2 during Asian session (low vol hours)
  is completely different from +2 at London open (high vol hours).
  Session-adjusted volume removes the systematic time-of-day bias.
  This is a MAJOR improvement over raw z-scores for FX specifically.
  Implementation: maintain rolling 20-day per-hour average.

[C5] vol_momentum_divergence  (price moving but volume disagreeing?)
  price_dir_5  = sign(close - close.shift(5))
  vol_dir_5    = sign(volume.rolling(5).mean() - volume.rolling(10).mean())
  formula      = (price_dir_5 × vol_dir_5).astype(float)   → {-1, 0, +1}
  range        = [-1, +1]
  -1 = price trending up while volume is FALLING = distribution.
  +1 = price and volume agree in direction = healthy trend.
  The disagreement is the most important divergence signal.

[C6] vol_efficiency  (price movement per unit of volume)
  price_5  = abs(close - close.shift(5)) / (ATR(14) + EPS)
  vol_5    = volume.rolling(5).mean() / (vol_30_avg + EPS)
  formula  = price_5 / (vol_5 + EPS)
  range    = [0, ~4]  clipped
  High = efficient thin-volume move (suspicious — may snap back).
  Low = lots of volume but little price progress = thick absorption happening.
  This is the inverse of the market's friction coefficient.

─── GROUP D: INSTITUTIONAL DETECTION (5 features) ───────────────────────

[D1] vol_at_vwap_z  (volume surge specifically at VWAP levels)
  at_vwap   = abs(close - vwap_50) < (0.2 × ATR(14))
  vol_vwap  = volume.where(at_vwap, 0)
  formula   = rolling_z(vol_vwap, 50)
  range     = [-2, +3]  clipped
  Institutional icebergs often execute near VWAP (minimizes price impact).
  High vol AT VWAP level = iceberg likely executing there.

[D2] institutional_footprint  (3-component iceberg signature)
  comp_a  = vol_at_vwap_z > 1.5           [high vol at fair value]    [0/1]
  comp_b  = abs(vwap_dev_atr) < 0.3       [price close to VWAP]      [0/1]
  comp_c  = body_ratio < 0.25             [small body despite activity] [0/1]
  formula = comp_a + comp_b + comp_c      → [0, 1, 2, 3]
  True iceberg leaves ALL THREE: executes near VWAP, large volume,
  price barely moves. The composite is the institutional fingerprint.

[D3] vwap_band_walk  (sustained extension above VWAP sigma bands)
  formula  = sum(max(0, vwap_sigma - 1.0), last 10 bars)
  range    = [0, ~6]
  Price walking above VWAP+1σ persistently = institutional buying
  continuously pushing price above fair value. Sustained walk = strong trend.
  Reverting from walk = rotation signal.

[D4] price_vol_divergence  (move without participation)
  price_move = abs(close.pct_change(5))
  vol_5_rel  = volume.rolling(5).mean() / (vol_30_avg + EPS)
  formula    = price_move / (vol_5_rel + EPS)  then z-scored
  range      = [-3, +3]
  Positive z-score = large price move on below-average volume = thin-air move.
  High values = suspect move, likely to reverse.

[D5] absorption_at_level  (vol spike + small body at any level)
  formula  = vol_z_30 × (body_ratio < 0.25).astype(float)
  range    = [-3, +3]
  High positive = elevated volume with tiny body = someone absorbing at current level.
  Not limited to VWAP — detects absorption at any price point.

─── GROUP E: VWAP STRUCTURAL (5 features) ────────────────────────────────

[E1] vwap_distance_pct  (raw % distance, different from ATR-normalized)
  formula  = (close - vwap_50) / (vwap_50 + EPS) × 100
  range    = [-0.5%, +0.5%]  for FX
  Absolute % provides context ATR-normalized version cannot.

[E2] above_vwap_streak  (consecutive bars above/below VWAP)
  formula  = signed consecutive count: positive if above, negative if below
  range    = [-30, +30]  clipped at ±20
  Long positive streak = sustained buy-side control.

[E3] vwap_mean_reversion_z  (overextension relative to VWAP sigma history)
  formula  = rolling_z(abs(vwap_sigma), 50)
  range    = [-2, +3]
  Detects when VWAP deviation is historically extreme for this instrument.

  formula  = sign(vwap_dev_atr) × sign(cum_delta_20_z)
  range    = {-1, 0, +1}
  +1 = price above VWAP AND delta positive = institutional buying confirming position.
  -1 = price above VWAP BUT delta negative = hidden distribution (classic divergence).

[E5] vol_surge_at_extreme  (high vol when far from VWAP)

  range    = [-3, +3]

  Combined with direction: high vol far from VWAP in direction of VWAP = mean reversion.
  High vol far from VWAP away from VWAP = breakout continuation.

  Directional call is inappropriate during absorption.

HEAD 4 — vwap_interaction  [4 classes]
  0 = rejection   1 = testing   2 = reclaim   3 = walk
  PRIMARY FEATURES: vwap_reclaim_quality, vwap_rejection_quality,

  LABEL LOGIC:
    walk      : abs(above_vwap_streak) >= 8 AND vwap_band_walk > 1.0
    reclaim   : vwap_reclaim_quality > 0.3 (quality cross above with vol)
    rejection : vwap_rejection_quality > 0.3 (quality cross below with vol)
    testing   : vwap_touch_density > 3 (returning to VWAP repeatedly)
                OR abs(vwap_dev_atr) < 0.2 (hovering at VWAP)
  WHY: These 4 states define the complete VWAP interaction vocabulary.
  Walk = sustained institutional directional control.
  Reclaim = bullish structural shift — buyers took back fair value.
  Rejection = bearish structural shift.
  Testing = indecision AT fair value — breakout imminent.

HEAD 5 — volume_trend  [3 classes]
  0 = declining   1 = stable   2 = building
  PRIMARY FEATURES: vol_trend, vol_momentum_divergence, vol_z_30 vs vol_z_100
  LABEL LOGIC:
    building  : vol_trend > 0.5 AND vol_z_30 > vol_z_100 (recent > long-term)
    declining : vol_trend < -0.5 AND vol_z_30 < vol_z_100
    stable    : everything in between
  WHY SEPARATE FROM VOLUME_CHARACTER:
    Character = magnitude (how much right now).
    Trend = direction of change (is it growing or shrinking?).
    A declining but still-high volume = distribution (character: surge, trend: declining).
    A building but still-low volume = accumulation forming (character: absence, trend: building).
    The combination tells a richer story than either alone.

  0 = avoid   1 = wait   2 = act
  PRIMARY FEATURES: synthesis of all above
  LABEL LOGIC:
    act  : (institutional_pressure != neutral)
           AND (vwap_interaction in (reclaim, walk))
           AND (volume_character in (surge, normal — not climax_surge))
           AND (volume_trend == building OR stable)
           AND cum_delta_vwap_align == +1  (delta confirms VWAP position)
           AND abs(vwap_sigma) < 2.0  (not already at extreme = risk:reward OK)
    avoid: institutional_footprint >= 2  (iceberg absorbing = do not trade)
           OR volume_character == absence  (no one participating)
           OR vol_momentum_divergence == -1  (price/vol disagreeing)
           OR vwap_zone in (above_2sd, below_2sd) AND not walk  (overextended)
    wait : everything else
